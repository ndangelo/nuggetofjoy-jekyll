<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background" style="font-size:28px">PROJECT BRIEF</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>How connected are you with what you eat?</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"backgroundColor":"pale-pink"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-pale-pink-background-color has-background">YOUR RESEARCH</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Choose a meal that holds a special significance to you. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Find the ingredients for this meal. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Catalog the ingredients in your dish. What are they? Where did they originate? </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Examine the packaging and trace everything used in the box to its origination. (This will require research). </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">For example, if maltodextrin is in one of your purchased ingredients, how is it made? Who makes it? Where? Use all this information to create a thought-provoking visual diagram portraying the "web" that is your favorite meal <strong>(it could be a very tangled web)</strong>. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">In addition to showing the connected geography of your meal, this visual diagram must also display its approximate carbon footprint through forms that vary in size or color.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-pale-cyan-blue-background-color has-background"><strong>SOME THINGS TO CONSIDER</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It would be best to utilize each ingredient's packaging to help dictate the form and color of the symbols, icons, maps, overlays, size, and spatial relationships to portray the story of your favorite meal best. This project's final format and medium is up to your interests and discretion (e.g., poster, website, booklet, etc.).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">START HERE</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Write a reflection essay as a deliverable about what you learned about the carbon footprint of the food they eat and how. This project will allow you to explore the connection between agriculture and climate change.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"backgroundColor":"pale-cyan-blue"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-pale-cyan-blue-background-color has-background">CONTINUE.....</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your project can be an infographic on posters to, pizza boxes, recipe cards to life-size cereal packaging. It would be best if you chose a subject you have some connection to. The end goal is the depiction of the message and content.</p>
