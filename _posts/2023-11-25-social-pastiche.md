<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Design a poster that is a pastiche of a historical design movement. Next, choose a current social, cultural, or political topic and communicate it using the historical design style. The historical design movement's social, cultural, or political ideologies should inspire finished designs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">PROJECT BRIEF</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Research design history to identify and investigate movements stylistically: typography, colors, composition, etc.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Define meanings for pastiche and poster.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Define an event, topic, or idea correlating modern social ideology with the historical design movement.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Develop sketches exploring the contextual meaning of graphic and style elements.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Create, refine, and present a final conceptual design</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:list -->
