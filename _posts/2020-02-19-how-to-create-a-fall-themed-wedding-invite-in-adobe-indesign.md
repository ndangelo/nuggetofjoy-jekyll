<h2>What You’ll Need to Create Your Invite</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We’ll be using&nbsp;<strong>Adobe InDesign</strong>&nbsp;to set up the main layout, and dipping into&nbsp;<strong>Illustrator</strong>&nbsp;for editing the leaf graphics, so make sure you have access to both of these programs. You’ll also need to download a few fonts and graphics to give you a foundation for creating your invitation:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li><a href="https://graphicriver.net/item/autumn-leaves-vector/3047373" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Autumn leaves vector</a></li><li><a href="https://pixabay.com/en/vintage-texture-vintage-paper-retro-2714966/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Textured paper background</a></li><li><a href="https://www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/jacques-francois" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Jacques Francois</a>&nbsp;font</li><li><a href="https://www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/England-Hand-DB" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">England Hand</a>&nbsp;font&nbsp;</li><li><a href="https://www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/im-fell-flowers-2" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">IM FELL FLOWERS 2</a>&nbsp;font</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Save the images to a folder you can easily find, and install the fonts onto your computer.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One thing that many people forget about when they are trying to design their own wedding invitations is the envelope! Envelopes come in a variety of standard sizes, whereas invites can vary widely in size, so it’s always a good idea to pin down your envelope size first, before you size your invitation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We’ll be setting up the invitation card at a standard size of 109 mm by 154 mm. To accommodate this, you’ll need to buy envelopes which are 4.75 in by 6.5 in when folded and closed. These are sometimes known as ‘6 Baronial’ envelopes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>1.&nbsp;How to Set Up the Invite in InDesign</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open up&nbsp;<strong>InDesign</strong>&nbsp;and go to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; New &gt; Document.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Keep the&nbsp;<strong>Intent</strong>&nbsp;set to&nbsp;<strong>Print</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Number of Pages</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>1</strong>. Uncheck the&nbsp;<strong>Facing Pages</strong>&nbsp;box.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the&nbsp;<strong>Width</strong>&nbsp;of the page to<strong>&nbsp;109 mm</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Height</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>154 mm.&nbsp;</strong>Add&nbsp;<strong>Margins</strong>&nbsp;of&nbsp;<strong>7 mm</strong>&nbsp;and a&nbsp;<strong>Bleed</strong>&nbsp;of&nbsp;<strong>5 mm</strong>. Then head up and click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/1.16.jpg" alt="new document"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Expand the&nbsp;<strong>Layers</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Layers)</strong>&nbsp;and double-click on&nbsp;<strong><em>Layer 1</em></strong>&nbsp;to open the&nbsp;<strong>Layer Options&nbsp;</strong>window. Rename the layer&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>&nbsp;and click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose&nbsp;<strong>New Layer</strong>&nbsp;from the panel’s drop-down menu (at top-right). Name this second layer&nbsp;<strong><em>Paper</em></strong>, and click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a further two new layers, firstly&nbsp;<strong><em>Typography</em></strong>&nbsp;and finally&nbsp;<strong><em>Leaves</em></strong>&nbsp;at the top of the sequence.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/1.26.jpg" alt="layer options"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock all the layers except&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>, and click on this layer to activate it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/1.2.1-4.jpg" alt="background layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>2.&nbsp;How to Create an Autumnal Backdrop for Your Invite</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can create the gradient backdrop for your invite using any pairing of colors you wish. To create the colors as used in the invite example here, expand the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Swatches)&nbsp;</strong>and choose&nbsp;<strong>New Color Swatch&nbsp;</strong>from the panel’s drop-down menu.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Name the swatch&nbsp;<strong>Cream</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Type</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Process</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Mode</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>CMYK</strong>. Set the percentage levels to&nbsp;<strong>C=9 M=16 Y=30 K=0</strong>. Then click&nbsp;<strong>Add</strong>, and&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a further two new CMYK swatches:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li><strong>Forest Green: C=75 M=45 Y=68 K=33</strong></li><li><strong>Burgundy: C=31 M=100 Y=76 K=46</strong></li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.18.jpg" alt="burgundy swatch"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>&nbsp;layer still active, take the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangle Tool (M)</strong>&nbsp;and drag across the whole page, extending the shape up to the edge of the bleed on all sides.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel, set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;of the rectangle to<strong>&nbsp;Forest Green.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.27.jpg" alt="forest green"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the shape selected, head up to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Effects &gt; Gradient Feather.</strong>&nbsp;Set the&nbsp;<strong>Angle</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>-90</strong>, and move the dark gradient stop about halfway along the slider.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.2.15.jpg" alt="gradient feather"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the shape and&nbsp;<strong>Edit &gt; Copy, Edit &gt; Paste</strong>. Then&nbsp;<strong>Right-click</strong>&nbsp;on the copy and choose&nbsp;<strong>Transform &gt; Flip Vertical.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.33.jpg" alt="flip vertical"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Position the flipped copy over the top of the original and switch its&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Burgundy</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.3.1.jpg" alt="burgundy swatch"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>&nbsp;layer and unlock the next layer up,&nbsp;<strong><em>Paper</em></strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangle Frame Tool (F)</strong>&nbsp;to drag across the whole page, and go to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Place</strong>. Choose the&nbsp;<a href="https://pixabay.com/en/vintage-texture-vintage-paper-retro-2714966/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">paper background image</a>&nbsp;you downloaded earlier, and click&nbsp;<strong>Open</strong>. Allow it to fill the whole frame.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.43.jpg" alt="paper image"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<h3>Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the image frame selected, go to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Effects &gt; Transparency.</strong>&nbsp;Set the&nbsp;<strong>Mode</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Multiply&nbsp;</strong>and reduce the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>90%.&nbsp;</strong></p>

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.5-1.jpg" alt="multiply"/></figure>

<h2>3.&nbsp;How to Format Elegant Typography for Your Invite</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the rulers visible&nbsp;<strong>(View &gt; Show Rulers)</strong>, drag a guideline down from the top ruler to&nbsp;<strong>52 mm.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.16.jpg" alt="guide line"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Take the&nbsp;<strong>Type Tool (T)&nbsp;</strong>and drag to create a text frame that extends from the left-hand margin to the right-hand margin. Type in&nbsp;<em>‘Please Join’</em>, and allow the baseline of the text to sit on the guideline.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From either the top&nbsp;<strong>Controls</strong>&nbsp;panel or the&nbsp;<strong>Character</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Type &amp; Tables &gt; Character)</strong>, set the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>England Hand, Size 11 pt</strong>&nbsp;and set the text to&nbsp;<strong>Align Center.</strong>&nbsp;Set the&nbsp;<strong>Font Color&nbsp;</strong>to&nbsp;<strong>Cream</strong>&nbsp;from the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.1.1.jpg" alt="please join text"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a second text frame below the first, typing in&nbsp;<em>‘Name 1’&nbsp;</em>in uppercase letters, and setting the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Jacques Francois, Size 13 pt</strong>&nbsp;and increasing the&nbsp;<strong>Tracking</strong>&nbsp;(space between all characters) to&nbsp;<strong>110</strong>. Set the&nbsp;<strong>Font Color</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Cream</strong>, as before.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.26.jpg" alt="name"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Copy</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Paste</strong>&nbsp;this name text frame, moving it below with a gap in between and adjusting the text to read&nbsp;<em>‘Name 2’.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.2.1-5.jpg" alt="name "/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set a text frame between the names reading&nbsp;<em>‘and’</em>, setting the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>England Hand.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.2.2.jpg" alt="england hand"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set another text frame below<em>&nbsp;‘Name 2’,</em>&nbsp;reading<em>&nbsp;‘to celebrate the occasion of their wedding’&nbsp;</em>and set in&nbsp;<strong>England Hand</strong>, as before.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.2.3.jpg" alt="england hand"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a text frame below the previous. Place your type cursor in the frame and set the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>IM FELL FLOWERS 2.</strong>&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then go to&nbsp;<strong>Window &gt; Type &amp; Tables &gt; Glyphs,</strong>&nbsp;and select a decorative glyph from the panel, like the leaf style pictured. Double-click to insert it. You can insert two opposite-facing glyphs to create a balanced divider.</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Revised1.jpg" alt="glyphs"/></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then&nbsp;<strong>Copy</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Paste</strong>&nbsp;the decorative divider text frame below.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Revised2.jpg" alt="glyphs"/></figure>

<h3>Step 5</h3>

<h2>4.&nbsp;How to Add Autumnal Leaves to Your Invite</h2>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open up the&nbsp;<a href="https://graphicriver.net/item/autumn-leaves-vector/3047373" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">autumn leaves vector</a>&nbsp;in&nbsp;<strong>Illustrator</strong>. Isolate one of the leaves in the illustration and set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;to a solid black swatch. Then&nbsp;<strong>Edit &gt; Copy</strong>&nbsp;the leaf.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Your invitation design is finished—great job! You can skip ahead to Section 5 below to find out how to export your design. <!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To do this, first identify which layer or layers you want to apply the foil design too. Let’s say you want to apply the foil to the leaves in the design, for example.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unlock the layer, and then create a new swatch from the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel. Name the swatch<strong>&nbsp;FOIL SPOT,</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Type</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Spot</strong>. Set the levels below to&nbsp;<strong>100% Magenta.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.35.jpg" alt="foil spot"/></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Apply this&nbsp;<strong>FOIL SPOT</strong>&nbsp;swatch to the elements you want to be pulled out in foil.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.3.1-4.jpg" alt="leaves foil"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then select all the foil elements and go to&nbsp;<strong>Window &gt; Output &gt; Attributes.</strong>&nbsp;Check the&nbsp;<strong>Overprint Fill</strong>&nbsp;box and/or&nbsp;<strong>Overprint Stroke.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.3.2.jpg" alt="overprint fill"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And you’re done!&nbsp;</p>

<h2>5.&nbsp;How to Export Your Invitation for Printing</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Head up to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Export</strong>, and choose&nbsp;<strong>Adobe PDF (Print)</strong>&nbsp;from the&nbsp;<strong>Format</strong>&nbsp;drop-down menu at the bottom of the&nbsp;<strong>Export</strong>&nbsp;window. Click&nbsp;<strong>Save</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the window that opens, choose&nbsp;<strong>Press Quality</strong>&nbsp;from the&nbsp;<strong>Preset</strong>&nbsp;menu at the top.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/5.15.jpg" alt="press quality"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then click on<strong>&nbsp;Marks and Bleeds</strong>&nbsp;in the window’s left-hand menu. Check&nbsp;<strong>All Printer’s Marks</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Use Document Bleed Settings</strong>, before clicking&nbsp;<strong>Export</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/5.1.1-4.jpg" alt="bleed and marks"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can send your exported PDF straight off to the printers—awesome work!</p>
