<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2.&nbsp;Color Replacement Tool Photoshop Settings</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before using the Color Replacement Tool in Photoshop, you need to open the image and select the color you want.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/1381/posts/39825/image-upload/selecting_the_color.jpg" alt="selecting the color "/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Advertisement</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now you can set the&nbsp;<strong>Size</strong>,&nbsp;<strong>Hardness</strong>, and&nbsp;<strong>Spacing</strong>&nbsp;of your brush.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/1381/posts/39825/image-upload/setting_up_color_replacement_brush.jpg" alt="setting up color replacement brush"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After that, you can select one of the&nbsp;<strong>Blending Modes</strong>:&nbsp;<strong>Color</strong>,&nbsp;<strong>Hue</strong>,&nbsp;<strong>Saturation</strong>,<strong>&nbsp;</strong>or&nbsp;<strong>Luminosity</strong>. The&nbsp;<strong>Color</strong>&nbsp;mode is a default setting that will change the hue and the saturation.&nbsp;Hue&nbsp;settings will only affect the hue,&nbsp;Saturation&nbsp;will only affect the saturation, and the&nbsp;Luminosity&nbsp;will match the brightness of the original color to the brightness of the new color, while hue and saturation will be unaffected.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you want to use Photoshop to replace one color with another, you should select&nbsp;<strong>Color,&nbsp;</strong>and in most cases, this will be your&nbsp;best choice.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/1381/posts/39825/image-upload/selecting_the_blending_mode_of_the_color_replacement_brush.jpg" alt="selecting the blending mode of the color replacement brush"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Want to see some examples of how to replace color in Photoshop? Take a look at some examples of using Photoshop to replace a color with brush blend modes below:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/1381/posts/39825/image-upload/color_replacement_tool_blending_modes.jpg" alt="color replacement tool blending modes"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The next setting is&nbsp;<strong>Sampling</strong>, and you can choose from three options:&nbsp;<strong>Continuous</strong>,&nbsp;<strong>Once</strong>, and&nbsp;<strong>Background Swatch</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Continuous Sampling</strong>&nbsp;will always look for the color you're painting over,&nbsp;<strong>Once Sampling&nbsp;</strong>will follow the color from the initial point of your brush, and&nbsp;<strong>Background Swatch</strong>&nbsp;will only paint over the areas that match your&nbsp;<strong>Background Color</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/1381/posts/39825/image-upload/replacement_tool_painting_modes.jpg" alt="replacement tool painting modes"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The&nbsp;<strong>Limits&nbsp;</strong>setting controls the "spread" of the colors. This option sets the boundary up to which it can affect the color.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Contiguous</strong>&nbsp;will affect only the sampled area of pixels where the crosshair of your brush is. It will not change the color of the adjacent areas that match the same color.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Discontiguous</strong>&nbsp;will change the color of the adjoining areas with a matching sampled color up to the spread of the crosshair boundaries.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Find Edges</strong>&nbsp;will find the edges in your image and stop as it approaches the edge. The color change will affect only the edges.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In most cases,&nbsp;<strong>Contiguous</strong>&nbsp;will be the best option to use.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/1381/posts/39825/image-upload/limit_settings.jpg" alt="limit settings"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 6</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The&nbsp;<strong>Tolerance</strong>&nbsp;setting controls how similar the color will be to your sampled color.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/1381/posts/39825/image-upload/tolerance_settings.jpg" alt="tolerance settings"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3.&nbsp;How to Use the Color Replacement Brush</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So, to use Photoshop to replace one color with another, let's set the following settings:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Brush Size: 160</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Mode: Color</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Sampling: Continuous</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Limits: Contiguous</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Tolerance: 75%</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After that, just paint over the areas where you want to change the color of your image.</p>
