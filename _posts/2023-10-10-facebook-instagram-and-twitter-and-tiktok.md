<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Before we embark on a full social media marketing campaign it is important that we experiment with creating posts using your brand.<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Create three text based posts on "X" or twitter and two text and image based. Remember to keep them branded.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Please create five branded posts (use your logo, colors and info from your creative brief.) for Facebook and Instagram. They should be image-based. Give them branded messages and a call to action (people need to know what to do when they see your ad) for each social media platform.)<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Create two video posts for (Your platform preference. Please make sure they are branded. Note: Utilize any tool you wish to format the video. You can use stock footage. Video you create with your phone etc.<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Please create five branded posts for Tiktok. I will present an example of a tiktok ad.<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Make sure the "look and feel" of your posts matches your style guides and make sure to include your logo in each post.<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Keep the videos short, interesting, and branded. Do not worry to much about video quality. Used the online or applications we covered in the last class as well.<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>You can also use Canva and adobe express to create your video ads.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>"Cross Post five of the posts you create for other platforms. Example: you can post an instagram account on your facebook page or X/twitter. These do not count toward the above tasks.<br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Besides the desktop applications mentioned above. <strong>DO NOT LOGIN TO YOUR SOCIAL MEDIA ACCOUNTS IN ROSENKRANS! </strong>PLEASE Use your phone and&nbsp; desktop applications for all your social media content.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
