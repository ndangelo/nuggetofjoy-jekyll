<p>With 1.3 billion monthly active users, and a projected 1.5 billion by 2026, Instagram is showing no signs of slowing down anytime soon. It has become a prime place for building brand awareness, showcasing personality, connecting with influencers, and promoting products. There are many ways you can incorporate Instagram into you <a href="https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/ultimate-social-media-marketing-guide">social media marketing</a> strategy, but it can be tough to know where to start.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the shining stars of social media to emerge from the last few years is undoubtedly the language-learning app <a href="https://www.instagram.com/duolingo/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Duolingo</a>. On the surface, the app is a straightforward educational service that helps users learn new languages through bite-sized lessons and quizzes. Gamification is introduced through "Streaks," which encourage daily use of the app.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Duolingo tapped into <span style="box-sizing: border-box; margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">the community culture of its target&nbsp;<strong>audience&nbsp;</strong>with a masterful campaign that brought its owl logo to life in hilarious skits, many of which poked fun at its</span> aggressive push-notification style.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="conclusion_of_the_best_instagram_marketing_examples">Conclusion of the Best Instagram Marketing Examples</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And we’re done! There’s 20 of the best Instagram marketing examples. It's important to note that all of these companies take different approaches in their&nbsp;<a href="https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/successful-instagram-marketing">Instagram marketing</a>, so&nbsp;<strong>there's no right way or wrong way</strong>&nbsp;to run your Instagram marketing strategy. But here are a few common denominators to keep in mind:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Use of video</strong>. It's no secret that short-form video is a favorite type of content among social media users, and if brands want to maintain high engagement they need to make sure their using&nbsp;<a href="https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/instagram-reels">Instagram Reels</a>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<a href="https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/instagram-stories">Instagram Stories</a>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Visual cohesion</strong>. Now there are a few business on this list that don't adhere to strict visual cohesion on their Instagram profiles, but in general this is a recommended rule to follow.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>People-forward</strong>. A majority of these Instagram marketing examples use the platform to showcase their teams and customers. Taking a people-forward approach as opposed to a product-forward approach is a great way to inspire trust and build a more engaged following.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Meme culture &amp; humor</strong>. Many of these profiles lean into humor for increasing engagement, appealing to a broader audience, and staying on top of certain visual and audio trends. Just make sure you know the references you're making inside and out. Nothing worse than trying to hop on a meme and failing!</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Influencer partnerships</strong>. Instagram is one of the best social media platforms for influencer marketing, and most of these brands take advantage of it. Influencers help you reach more consumers through a trusted voice and word-of-mouth.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>UGC</strong>. Lastly, Instagram is also a fantastic place to execute a&nbsp;<a href="https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/ugc-user-generated-content">UGC strategy</a>. When you're tagged or a branded hashtag is used (such as #ShotoniPhone), Instagram makes it easy to find these posts and reshare them — which is a self-fulfilling prophecy in that it encourages others to use your products and tag you in their posts, in the hope of being featured.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So<span style="box-sizing: border-box; margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">, whether through&nbsp;<a href="https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/influencer-marketing" target="_blank" rel="noopener">influencer marketing</a>, short-form video, or nostalgia leveraging</span>, each of these profiles is an excellent example of Instagram marketing's potential.</p>
