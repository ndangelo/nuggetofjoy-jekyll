<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Your brand needs attention. TikTok gives you a way to earn it.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>TikTok is a platform for promoting products, services, creative work, and personal brands through short-form video. The goal isn't simply to go viral. It's to make people notice your brand, understand what you offer, and remember you.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Three Ways to Promote Your Brand</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. Teach Something</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Give your audience a useful tip, tutorial, or demonstration. Make your content worth watching even for someone who has never heard of your brand.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Think:</strong> What can you teach in 20 seconds?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Example:</strong> A science educator turns an experiment into short-form video.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">2. Show Your Process</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Show how you make something, solve a problem, or develop an idea. People are interested in how things work.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Think:</strong> What happens behind the scenes of your brand?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Example:</strong> A visual science demonstration communicates an idea through action.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">3. Make People Remember You</h3>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Think:</strong> What makes your brand different?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Example:</strong> A science demonstration uses a visual surprise to capture attention.</p>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Five Rules for Better TikTok Videos</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true,"start":1} -->
<ol start="1" class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Hook them.</strong> Give viewers a reason to stop scrolling.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Keep it focused.</strong> One video, one main idea.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Show your brand.</strong> Use consistent colors, typography, and personality.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Edit with purpose.</strong> Use vertical video, readable text, captions, and intentional sound.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Give viewers a next step.</strong> Ask them to follow, comment, visit your website, or explore your work.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use music cleared for commercial use. TikTok's <a href="https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/music/pc/en">Commercial Music Library</a> is a useful resource.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How Do You Know It's Working?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Look beyond views. Pay attention to watch time, shares, comments, saves, profile visits, and website clicks. Choose metrics that match your goal.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Explore the <a href="https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/">TikTok Creative Center</a> for trends and creative inspiration.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>The takeaway:</strong> Don't just make videos. Make videos that communicate your brand.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Your Assignment: Promote Your Brand on TikTok</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create three short videos promoting your own brand. Each video must have a different purpose:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Video 1: Introduce it.</strong> What is your brand, and why should people care?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Video 2: Demonstrate it.</strong> Show your product, service, skills, or creative process.</li>
