<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Design Scenario</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">You’re a UX/UI designer at a startup developing a messenger app. Your team decided to increase market share by developing a smartwatch version.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Design Task</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Your design task is to complete the following:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Select the type of a platform for your design.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Create a design or prototype of a messenger app optimized for smartwatches.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Ensure the app is accessible, including for people with motor or visual disabilities.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Explain the rationale behind your smartwatch design.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Use the provided smartwatch templates to showcase your designs.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Bonus points for making your project submission interactive. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Evaluation criteria</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Your project will be evaluated using the following criteria:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Usability</strong>&nbsp;— Can users perform tasks safely, effectively, and efficiently while enjoying the experience?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Accessibility</strong>&nbsp;— Does the design comply with the WCAG’s requirements and is accessible to all users? Is the design inclusive to all users regardless of their background, race, age, gender, and ethnicity?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Visual design</strong>&nbsp;— Do designs effectively engage the user through strategic use of color, typography, and imagery?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Presentation</strong>&nbsp;— How effectively is the work presented, including the templates provided, the formatting of the copy, the links shared, and the explanation of the design rationale?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<p class="has-medium-font-size"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">For the theme, they chose a dark color scheme to complement the watch's black bezels, creating a cohesive and visually appealing interface. The colors are inspired by the standard Apple theme, ensuring familiarity and ease of use for users. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">They focused primarily on voice dictation for message input, believing it to be significantly easier and more efficient than typing on a smartwatch. This approach enhances the user experience by leveraging the natural convenience of voice commands in a compact and wearable device.</p>
