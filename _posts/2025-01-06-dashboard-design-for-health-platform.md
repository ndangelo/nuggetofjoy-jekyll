<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Showcase your user research and content strategy skills by designing a dashboard for the health &amp; wellness industry.</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">You are employed as a UX/UI designer by a company that provides health and wellness services. One of the objectives of your platform is to enable users to track their progress and monitor their well-being. With numerous parameters to consider, users can easily become overwhelmed. Conduct research to determine which data is most important and design a dashboard that effectively reflects it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Design Task</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your design task is to complete the following:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Select the platform and device type for your design.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Conduct research to understand user needs.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Design a dashboard that presents essential data.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Ensure the dashboard is informative, clear, and user-friendly.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Provide an explanation of your design decisions.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Evaluation criteria</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Your project will be evaluated using the following criteria:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Content</strong>&nbsp;— Does the content serve its purpose, have high quality, and contribute to the full functionality of the project?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Visual design</strong>&nbsp;— Do designs effectively engage the user through strategic use of color, typography, and imagery?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Usability</strong>&nbsp;— Can users perform tasks safely, effectively, and efficiently while enjoying the experience?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Presentation</strong>&nbsp;— How effectively is the work presented, including the templates provided, the formatting of the copy, the links shared, and the explanation of the design rationale?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>A minimum of 20 individual sections of functionality/display are required for the successful completion of this progress.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
