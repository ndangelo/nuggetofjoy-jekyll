<p><strong>Assignment: </strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li>Create four quadrants on four pages of your sketchbook</li><li>Find an object(s) that you think might inspire you to create a drawing</li><li>Within each quadrant create curves, lines, and doodles to develop a visual vocabulary to use later</li><li>Begin creating thumbnail sketches based of your "model, line/forms and sketches</li><li>Due date is TBD. Most if not all work will be completed in class.</li></ul>
