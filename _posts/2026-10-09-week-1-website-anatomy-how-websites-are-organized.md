<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key concepts</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A website is more than a collection of colors and images. Its regions have different jobs and help visitors understand where they are, what a page offers, and what they can do next.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Header:</strong>&nbsp;often contains the site name or logo and primary navigation.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Hero:</strong>&nbsp;the prominent opening area that introduces the page’s main message.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Main content:</strong>&nbsp;the information, services, stories, or work the page exists to communicate.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Footer:</strong>&nbsp;often contains secondary navigation, contact information, social links, or ownership details.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Not every website uses these regions in exactly the same way. The structure should fit the content and audience.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
