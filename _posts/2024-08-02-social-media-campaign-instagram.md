<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background"><strong>OVERVIEW</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">In this assignment, you will <strong>design and produce a social media campaign consisting of six Instagram graphic posts with a unifying graphic theme, style, and overarching message. </strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber","fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background has-medium-font-size"><strong>Note: The final posts should consist of three images and text, as well as three video-based posts. One of the image-based posts should include a slideshow.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Each Instagram post will have a graphic, a caption, a campaign hashtag, and post-specific hashtags. Your campaign topic can be anything related to social media marketing trends, design, or user experience (UX).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h1 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background"><strong>CREATE</strong></h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"backgroundColor":"pale-pink"} -->
<p class="has-pale-pink-background-color has-background"><strong>STEP 1</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Define your social media campaign topic, theme, and style. Include the following in your campaign description:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Topic<ul><li>The rationale for selecting this topic</li></ul><ul><li>Target audience.<em> Tip: be very specific and go beyond demographics. Consider shared behaviors, attitudes, and lifestyle.</em></li></ul><ul><li>Voice and tone. <em> Tip: use plain English that is accessible and inclusive. Make sure to avoid buzzwords and jargon.</em></li></ul><ul><li>Unifying color palette. <em>Tip: the </em><a href="https://color.adobe.com/create/color-wheel"><em>Adobe Color Wheel</em></a><em> may help select</em></li></ul> colors. Other unifying elements, such as font(s), are used in<ul><li>Graphic style.</li></ul><!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Campaign hashtag.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>A hashtag is a pound sign (i.e., #) followed by a keyword or group of keywords with no spaces like this: #hashtag. </strong>On Instagram and other hashtag-friendly <span style="box-sizing: border-box; margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">platforms around the web, the <strong>hashtag becomes a link,</strong> allowing all content tagged with the same hashtag to</span> be connected. You can follow hashtags on Instagram to discover content related to your interests. Hashtags can also be used to group your content. If you’re embarking on a special campaign or project, you can use a unique hashtag to tie all your related posts together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"backgroundColor":"pale-pink"} -->
<p class="has-pale-pink-background-color has-background"><strong>STEP 2</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Review the research you did and the images you collected. Design six social media posts that align with your campaign theme. Include the following items in your post: An image or collage of images with or without words for your post graphic. Text for your caption. Keep it short and eliminate any unnecessary words. <br></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Campaign hashtag. Tip: You may put this in the caption body or at the end. If you include the campaign hashtag in a list of hashtags, it should be prominent, typically placed first or last. Other hashtags. Tips: The best hashtags are relatively short, easy to remember, specific, and relevant to your target audience. You can include up to 30 hashtags in a single post, but limit the number of hashtags you use because too many look<em style="color: initial; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, &quot;Segoe UI&quot;, Roboto, Oxygen-Sans, Ubuntu, Cantarell, &quot;Helvetica Neue&quot;, sans-serif;"> spammy.</em></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

Include the following:<ul><li>Campaign theme and style;<ul><li>Campaign topic</li></ul><ul><li>Campaign theme and style guidelines: target audience, voice and tone, color palette, fonts, other graphic elements</li></ul><ul><li>Campaign hashtag</li></ul></li></ul><ul><li>For each of the six Instagram</li></ul> posts include the following<span style="color: initial;">:</span><ul><li><ul><li>Title</li></ul><ul><li>Purpose of the post</li></ul><ul><li>Intended message</li></ul><ul><li>The graphic</li></ul><ul><li>Caption with hashtags and optional emojis</li></ul></li></ul><!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>A list of sources you used for research. Include the author, title, date, publication or website, URL, and whatever else is needed to identify the source fully.</li>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-pale-cyan-blue-background-color has-background"><strong>The Sample Assignment&nbsp;</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"backgroundColor":"pale-pink"} -->
<p class="has-pale-pink-background-color has-background"><strong>Campaign theme and style&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Topic: UX Visual Design Principles&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Visual Design is crucial for engaging users on social media, especially on graphic-centric platforms like Instagram.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Target audience. 18-34 year olds with an interest in digital design.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Voice and tone: </strong>Professional, technically correct, and engaging. Stated in plain English so that it is accessible to someone with no formal training in the subject area.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Unifying elements:&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Color palette:</strong> a limited color palette will be used for this campaign.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Font: </strong>Acier BAT Text Solid&nbsp;</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><br><strong>Campaign hashtag:</strong> #UXdesign&nbsp;</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Instagram Post 1&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Title: </strong>Radial Balance&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Purpose of the design: </strong>This is an Instagram main feed post designed to demonstrate how Radial Balance can be used to make a design more interesting and engaging by indicating circular motion.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Intended message:</strong> Motion can be created with a "static" image.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":65964,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none","align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/image-141-1024x1024.png" alt="Radial Balance" class="wp-image-65964"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><br><strong>Caption: </strong>This seems like it's moving but it's not. Static images don't need to be over complicated or boring to have an impact. This image uses radial balance to organize the complexity of all the flowing lines. #radial-balance #UXdesign&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Instagram Post 2&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Title: </strong>Figure-Ground&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Purpose of the design: This Instagram main feed post demonstrates the Gestalt principle of Figure &amp; Ground when the mind distinguishes an object from the surrounding area.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Intended message: You can exploit common figure-ground perception conventions to “confuse” the viewer and add interest, even when using simple objects like flat squares.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":65966,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none","align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/image-143-1024x1024.png" alt="Figure and Ground" class="wp-image-65966"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><br><strong>Caption: </strong>Look at this figure &amp; ground illustration for a moment. What are the figures and what is the ground (surrounding area)? It may seem to change, with the smallest squares being the figures on the square fields, and then switch so that the larger squares with the squares inside become the figures. <strong>#gestalt #UXdesign&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Instagram Post 3&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Title: </strong>Proximity&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Purpose of the design: This is an Instagram main feed post demonstrating how Proximity can be used to encourage the viewer to group similar objects together and incentivize them to engage further.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Intended message: Proximity can be used to simplify information by grouping objects together. We don't really think about this process unless we are specifically prompted to engage. In this&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":65967,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none","align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/image-144-1024x1024.png" alt="Proximity" class="wp-image-65967"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><br><strong>Caption: </strong>How many objects are in this photo? Because of the Law of Proximity, we first perceive this image as two objects: a single silver ball getting ready to hit an object composed of four identical silver balls. Because of the question, TWO OR FIVE? you were prompted to also think about the five identical silver balls. Grouping things like this helps us simplify our world and remove unnecessary complexity. #<strong>gestalt #RCSCdesign&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
