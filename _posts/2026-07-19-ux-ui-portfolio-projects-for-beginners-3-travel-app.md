<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-pale-cyan-blue-background-color has-background">Project Overview</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For this project, you will design a <strong>mobile travel app</strong> that helps users plan and organize a trip from beginning to end.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your app should allow a user to <strong>search for and book travel, discover activities, and organize their entire trip in one place</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You will choose an existing travel brand to redesign/reimagine <strong>or create your own travel brand</strong>. Your goal is not simply to make attractive screens. You must demonstrate a thoughtful <strong>UI/UX process</strong> that solves real problems for travelers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Timeline:</strong> 1–2 weeks<br><strong>Platform:</strong> iOS or Android<br><strong>Primary Tool:</strong> Figma or another approved UI/UX design tool</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Design Challenge</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your app should allow users to:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Find and compare <strong>travel deals</strong>. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Book a <strong>flight</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Book a <strong>hotel</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Book a <strong>rental car</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Find and select <strong>activities</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Organize plans into an <strong>itinerary</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Receive <strong>notifications</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background"><strong>Deliverables</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Research</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Research at least <strong>two existing travel apps</strong>, including the provided examples such as KAYAK and Expedia.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Identify:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What works well</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What could be improved</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Features you would like to incorporate into your app</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your research should influence your design decisions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Target Audience</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Define who your app is designed for.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Consider:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Who are your users?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What are their travel needs?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What problems might they have when planning a trip?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Your Design Solution</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Identify <strong>three unique features or improvements</strong> that will make your app different from existing travel apps.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For each feature, explain:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What it does</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What user problem it solves</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These features should be reflected in your final design.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
