<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1.&nbsp;How to Create a Web Document in InDesign</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open&nbsp;<strong>InDesign</strong>&nbsp;and go to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; New &gt; Document.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the intent set to&nbsp;<strong>Web</strong>, set the&nbsp;<strong>Width</strong>&nbsp;of the page to&nbsp;<strong>435 px</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Height</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>1300 px.&nbsp;</strong>This is a good size for sharing on sites like Pinterest.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the&nbsp;<strong>Margins</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>36 px</strong>, and click&nbsp;<strong>Create</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/1.1-5.jpg" alt="new document"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Expand the&nbsp;<strong>Layers</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Layers)</strong>, and double-click on&nbsp;<strong><em>Layer 1</em></strong>, renaming it&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create four more new layers—<strong><em>Arrows, Rocket, Icons</em></strong>&nbsp;and finally,&nbsp;<strong><em>Type</em></strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then lock all the layers except&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/1.2-4.jpg" alt="type layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2.&nbsp;How to Create a Celestial Backdrop&nbsp;</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangle Frame Tool (F)</strong>&nbsp;to create an image frame across the whole page.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>File &gt; Place</strong>, navigate to the&nbsp;<strong>Space Backgrounds</strong><span style="color:#cf2e2e" class="tadv-color"> (Use a free version of our choice. Nasa has a lot of free images you can download)</span> image. Click&nbsp;<strong>Open</strong>&nbsp;and allow it to fill up the whole frame.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.1-4.jpg" alt="placed image"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Switch to the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangle Tool (M)</strong>&nbsp;and drag across the top of the page to create a rough square shape.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Color &gt; Swatches)</strong>,&nbsp;set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;to<strong>&nbsp;[Black].</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.2-3.jpg" alt="black shape"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the shape selected, go to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Effects &gt; Gradient Feather.</strong>&nbsp;Set the&nbsp;<strong>Type</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Linear</strong>&nbsp;and allow the gradient to extend from darker at the top to transparent towards the bottom of the shape. Click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.2.1-3.jpg" alt="gradient feather"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the shape and&nbsp;<strong>Edit &gt; Copy, Edit &gt; Paste</strong>&nbsp;it. Then, on the copy,&nbsp;<strong>Right-Click &gt; Transform &gt; Flip Vertical.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.3-3.jpg" alt="flip vertical"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Move to the bottom of the page, and extend the height of the shape upwards to about halfway up the page.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.3.1-3.jpg" alt="background layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open the&nbsp;<a href="https://image-control-storage.s3.amazonaws.com/space_shuttle_the_launch_to_the_sky.zip" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label="space shuttle&nbsp; (opens in a new tab)"><strong>space shuttle</strong>&nbsp;</a>illustration in&nbsp;<strong>Illustrator</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select only the rocket shape, plus one of the transparent layers of smoke along the bottom of the image, pictured below. Then&nbsp;<strong>Copy</strong>&nbsp;these.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.4-3.jpg" alt="rocket graphic"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a new Illustrator document, and&nbsp;<strong>Paste</strong>&nbsp;these elements onto the page, before going to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Save As</strong>&nbsp;and saving this as an Illustrator EPS file.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.4.1-3.jpg" alt="rocket graphic"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Return to your&nbsp;<strong>InDesign</strong>&nbsp;document and lock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>&nbsp;layer. Unlock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Rocket</em></strong>&nbsp;layer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create an image frame using the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangle Frame Tool (F)</strong>&nbsp;at the bottom of the page and&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Place</strong>, choosing the edited rocket EPS image.&nbsp;<strong>Open</strong>, and make sure the rocket image is centered on the page.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.5-4.jpg" alt="rocket image"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3.&nbsp;How to Create a Starry Header for Your Infographic</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Rocket</em></strong>&nbsp;layer and unlock the top layer,&nbsp;<strong><em>Type</em></strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Zoom in on the top of the page, take the<strong>&nbsp;Type Tool (T)</strong>, and create a text frame across the top-center of the page.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Type in ‘SPACE’, and from either the top&nbsp;<strong>Controls</strong>&nbsp;panel or the&nbsp;<strong>Character</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Paragraph</strong>&nbsp;panels&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Type &amp; Tables &gt; Character/Paragraph)</strong>, set the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>BW Modelica Black, Size 100 pt, Tracking 100</strong>, and&nbsp;<strong>Align Center.</strong>&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel, set the&nbsp;<strong>Font Color</strong>&nbsp;to<strong>&nbsp;[Paper].</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.1-6.jpg" alt="character panel"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Add two text frames above and below ‘SPACE’, set in&nbsp;<strong>BW Modelica ExtraBold, Size 30 pt, Tracking 230</strong>,&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Align Center.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The first should read ‘THE FUTURE OF’ and the bottom simply ‘TRAVEL’.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.2-4.jpg" alt="text frame"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the ‘SPACE’ text frame and go to Type > Create Outlines to vectorize the text. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.3-3.jpg" alt="create outlines"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the&nbsp;<strong>Color Fill</strong>&nbsp;of the outlined text to<strong>&nbsp;[None].&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.3.1-3.jpg" alt="outlined text"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then, go to File > Place, choose an image from the Space Backgrounds folder (I’ve chosen ‘04.jpg’ for contrast), and Open it, allowing it to fill the whole word.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.3.2-6.jpg" alt="space image"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">4.&nbsp;How to Build Up Graphics on Your Layout</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Type</em></strong>&nbsp;layer and unlock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Arrows</em></strong>&nbsp;layer.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the&nbsp;<strong>Line Tool ()</strong>&nbsp;to create a vertical line running from the top of the rocket to about a quarter of the way down the page.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the&nbsp;<strong>Stroke Color&nbsp;</strong>to&nbsp;<strong>[Paper]</strong>. From the&nbsp;<strong>Stroke</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Stroke)</strong>, set the&nbsp;<strong>Weight</strong>&nbsp;of the line to&nbsp;<strong>20 pt</strong>, and add a&nbsp;<strong>Triangle</strong>&nbsp;shape to the&nbsp;<strong>End</strong>&nbsp;of the line.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.1-3.jpg" alt="arrow stroke"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the line selected, go to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Effects &gt; Gradient Feather.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Add a&nbsp;<strong>Linear</strong>&nbsp;gradient at a&nbsp;<strong>90 Degree Angle</strong>, allowing the arrow to be more transparent towards the base.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.2-3.jpg" alt="gradient feather"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><b>Please copy</b> and <strong>Paste</strong> the arrow, moving it over to the right side of the page, and shortening it so that it is positioned about halfway up the page.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.3-3.jpg" alt="pasted arrow"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Paste</strong>&nbsp;more arrows across the page, in the formation shown below, shortening them as needed.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.3.1-11.jpg" alt="arrows pasted"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open up the<strong>&nbsp;</strong><a href="https://image-control-storage.s3.amazonaws.com/space-icons.zip" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label="space icon set (opens in a new tab)"><strong>space icon set</strong></a>&nbsp;in&nbsp;<strong>Illustrator</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Right-Click &gt; Ungroup</strong>&nbsp;the image, so you can select icons individually.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.4-8.jpg" alt="ungroup icons"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the ‘Mars’ icon on the fourth row down, and&nbsp;<strong>Edit &gt; Copy</strong>&nbsp;it.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.4.1-4.jpg" alt="mars icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Back in your&nbsp;<strong>InDesign</strong>&nbsp;document, lock the&nbsp;<em><strong>Arrows</strong></em>&nbsp;layer and unlock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Icons</em></strong>&nbsp;layer.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Edit &gt; Paste</strong>&nbsp;the icon onto the page, and move it so that it sits at the top of the central arrow.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.5-4.jpg" alt="pasted icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel, choose&nbsp;<strong>New Color Swatch</strong>&nbsp;from the panel’s main menu. Create a new RGB swatch,&nbsp;<strong>R=41 G=156 B=186</strong>. Click&nbsp;<strong>Add</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Done</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.5.1-4.jpg" alt="new rgb swatch"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Apply this swatch to the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;of the circle sitting behind the planet.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.5.2-4.jpg" alt="blue fill"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 6</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Head back to the Illustrator icons and select the ‘Rocket’ icon. <strong>Copy</strong> it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.6-2.jpg" alt="rocket icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Paste</strong>&nbsp;this directly onto your InDesign document, and position it at the top of the arrow on the far-right side of the page.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a new RGB swatch,&nbsp;<strong>R=239 G=79 B=77</strong>, and apply this to the circle.&nbsp;<strong>Right-Click &gt; Transform &gt; Rotate</strong>&nbsp;the rocket to a&nbsp;<strong>45</strong>&nbsp;degree angle.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.6.1-2.jpg" alt="rocket icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 7</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Continue to copy and paste more icons, ‘Astronaut’, ‘Orbit’, and ‘Earth’, onto the infographic, placing these in the positions shown below.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create new RGB swatches of your own choosing and apply these to each of the background circles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.7-2.jpg" alt="earth icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 8</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Isolate the figure of the ‘Alien’ on the icon image and copy and paste it onto the infographic, too. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.8-2.jpg" alt="alien icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Position it on the bottom-right of the layout.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.8.1-2.jpg" alt="alien graphic"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 9</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Icons</em></strong>&nbsp;layer and unlock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Type</em></strong>&nbsp;layer.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With your icons in place, you can now start to build up text on your infographic, to match each icon.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the&nbsp;<strong>Type Tool (T)</strong>&nbsp;and create a text frame to the right of the ‘Mars’ icon at the top. Type in a subtitle, and set the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to<strong>&nbsp;BW Modelica ExtraBold, Size 30 pt, Tracking 90</strong>,&nbsp;and<strong>&nbsp;Align Right.</strong>&nbsp;Set the&nbsp;<strong>Font Color</strong>&nbsp;to<strong>&nbsp;[Paper].</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.9-2.jpg" alt="sub-heading"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 10</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a second text frame below this, typing in your chosen fact, and setting the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>BW Modelica Bold, Size 12 pt, Tracking 60</strong>, and<strong>&nbsp;Align Right.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.10-2.jpg" alt="body text"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 11</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now you can select both the heading and body text frames and&nbsp;<strong>Copy</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Paste</strong>&nbsp;these, to populate your infographic with more text.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.11-3.jpg" alt="text alignment"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Adjust the text alignment as you go to suit their position on the page.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.11.1-2.jpg" alt="text frames"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 12</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you have any areas of the infographic where the text is difficult to read because of the background, you can create circles in<strong>&nbsp;[Black]&nbsp;</strong>using the&nbsp;<strong>Ellipse Tool (L).</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.12-3.jpg" alt="ellipse tool"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Go to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Effects &gt; Gradient Feather&nbsp;</strong>to apply a&nbsp;<strong>Radial</strong>&nbsp;gradient to these shapes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.12.1-2.jpg" alt="gradient"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then make sure to&nbsp;<strong>Right-Click &gt; Arrange &gt; Send to Back</strong>&nbsp;to push them behind the text.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.12.2-2.jpg" alt="send to back"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 13&nbsp;</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you want to make a feature of certain statistics, you can set numbers in large-size <strong>BW Modelica. </strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.13-3.jpg" alt="large type"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then go to&nbsp;<strong>Type &gt; Create Outlines.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:gallery {"linkTo":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-gallery has-nested-images columns-default is-cropped"><!-- wp:image {"id":56504,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.13.1.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-56504"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image --></figure>
<!-- /wp:gallery -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>File &gt; Place</strong>, choosing one of the space background images, and&nbsp;<strong>Open</strong>. This will really make the figure pop on the page.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
