<p>I will need to be made an administrator of your Facebook page. Please invite me: the class facebook page is <a href="https://www.facebook.com/cendeavorslab/">https://www.facebook.com/cendeavorslab/</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You'll need to be an admin to manage roles for your Page. Learn how to&nbsp;<a href="https://www.facebook.com/help/510247025775149">see what your role is</a>. There’s no limit to the number of people who can have a role on a Page. Learn more about&nbsp;<a href="https://www.facebook.com/help/289207354498410">what each Page role can do</a>.<br>Give Someone a Role If you’re an admin:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li>Click&nbsp;<strong>Settings</strong>&nbsp;at the top of your Page.</li><li>Click&nbsp;<strong>Page Roles</strong>&nbsp;in the left column.</li><li>Type a name or email in the box and select the person from the list that appears.</li><li>Click&nbsp;<strong>Editor</strong>&nbsp;to select a role from the dropdown menu.</li><li>Click&nbsp;<strong>Add</strong>&nbsp;and enter your password to confirm.</li></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Keep in mind that if you’re not friends with the person you’re adding, they’ll have to&nbsp;<a href="https://www.facebook.com/help/1021117938014211">accept your invite</a>&nbsp;before they can start helping you manage your Page. So you will have to send me a friend invite.</p>
