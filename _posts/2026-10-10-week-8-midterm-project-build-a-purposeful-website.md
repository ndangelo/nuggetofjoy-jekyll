<h2 class="wp-block-heading">90-minute class plan</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>0–10 min — Opening and visual warm-up:</strong>&nbsp;Introduce the learning goals, review the previous class, and discuss a relevant website example.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>10–25 min — Mini-lesson:</strong>&nbsp;Explain the key concepts and show examples. Keep the presentation focused on what students will apply.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>25–45 min — Instructor demonstration:</strong>&nbsp;Walk through the interactive CodePen or a live example. Pause for predictions and questions.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>45–70 min — Guided and independent practice:</strong>&nbsp;Students complete the lesson exercise, first following a short demonstration and then making their own decisions.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>70–83 min — Apply and critique:</strong>&nbsp;Students connect the skill to their website project, compare results with a partner, and make one improvement.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>83–90 min — Share-out and exit ticket:</strong>&nbsp;Review key takeaways and ask students to identify one thing they learned and one next step.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Learning objectives</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Define the intended audience and primary goal of a website.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Organize page content into a clear hierarchy.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Use a wireframe and visual system to guide implementation.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key concepts</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a responsive, multi-section website for a small business, artist, or creative professional. Your site should have a clear audience, a defined purpose, an understandable navigation or section structure, and a consistent visual direction.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before coding, be ready to explain:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Who the site is for.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What visitors should understand or do.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Which content is essential and which is secondary.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>How the design will work on desktop and mobile.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>How typography, color, imagery, and layout support the goal.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The goal is not to include every possible feature. The goal is to make deliberate choices that work together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
