<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key concepts</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Images establish context, emotion, and identity. Crop, focal point, aspect ratio, and color treatment can change how an image feels and how it works within a layout.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use images that support the content rather than choosing them only because they look attractive. Consider where the image will appear, how it will crop on mobile screens, whether you have permission to use it, and whether the file is optimized.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Alternative text should communicate meaningful information conveyed by an image when that information is not already available nearby. Decorative images may need empty alternative text so assistive technology can skip them.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
