<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key concepts</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Typography communicates tone and creates a path through information. Headings, subheadings, body text, labels, and captions should have distinct but related roles. Scale, weight, line height, and line length all affect readability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Color communicates hierarchy and mood, but color choices must maintain sufficient contrast. A beautiful palette is not successful if visitors cannot comfortably read its text.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A visual system should be consistent without making every element identical. Use a limited set of type styles, a deliberate color palette, and repeatable spacing decisions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
