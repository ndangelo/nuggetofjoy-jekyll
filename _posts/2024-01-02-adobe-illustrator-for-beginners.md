<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-5tze-how-to-use-the-curvature-tool">8. How to Use the Curvature Tool in Illustrator</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Although drawing with simple shapes can get you a long way, you'll also need to master some of the more complex drawing tools in Illustrator. Let's start with the&nbsp;<strong>Curvature Tool</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can create a fluid, curving line simply by choosing the&nbsp;<strong>Curvature Tool&nbsp;</strong>and then clicking to create anchor points along the curve.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/curvature_tool.jpg" alt="curvature tool in illustrator"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then you can go in and refine your curve by adjusting the guides for each anchor point.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/anchors.jpg" alt="adjusting anchors"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/curvature_straight.jpg" alt="straight lines with curvature tool"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Despite its name, you can also use the Curvature Tool in Illustrator to draw straight lines! Double-click as you create each anchor point to create a corner instead of a curve.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
