<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Let's look at how to combine two images in Photoshop using the Generative Fill tool! It's quick, easy, and extremely powerful. All while still offering the freedom to mask and edit your image perfectly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-rvqk-how-to-combine-two-images-in-photoshop">How to Blend Two Images in Photoshop Using AI</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-xqdn-step-1">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>First, place two images on top of each other.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","linkDestination":"custom"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0005_1-4.jpg" alt="place two images on top of each other"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-a93i-step-2">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the seam of the images using the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangular Marquee</strong>&nbsp;tool.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0004_2.jpg" alt="Select the seam of the images using the Rectangular Marquee tool"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-yknt-step-3">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Hit&nbsp;<strong>Generate&nbsp;</strong>in the context bar without filling in a prompt.&nbsp;Photoshop will connect and blend the two images in a way that makes the most sense.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You maybe have to hit generate multiple times to get a good result.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0003_3.jpg" alt="Hit Generate in the context bar without filling in a prompt"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-6kyw-step-4">Step 4 (Optional)</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Depending on the result, you can use the "Generative Fill" layers'&nbsp;Layer Mask&nbsp;to mask away and blend back some of the original images underneath.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In this example, we return the subject's original face while keeping the interesting hair design.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0002_4.jpg" alt="mask away any unwarted image"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-rvqk-how-to-combine-two-images-in-photoshop">How to Combine Two Images in Photoshop Using AI</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-rd9h-step-1">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Alternatively, you can use generative fill to connect and combine two images in Photoshop.&nbsp;Place the images you want to combine next to each other but leave an empty canvas section between them.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the space and part of both images using the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangular Marquee</strong>&nbsp;tool.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","linkDestination":"custom"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0001_5.jpg" alt="Select the empty space and part of both images using the Rectangular Marquee tool"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-2wno-step-2">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Hit Generate in the context bar without filling in a prompt. Photoshop will connect the two images, considering both lighting and perspective.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0000_6.jpg" alt="Hit Generate in the context bar without filling in a prompt"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-3w5y-thats-how-to-combine-two-images-in-photoshop-using-ai">That's How to Combine Two Images in Photoshop!</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Combining two images in Photoshop using the Generative Fill tool is a breeze. Just remember to hit Generate a few times until you get a happy result. And even if it's imperfect, don't worry - you can always use it as a jumping-off point! With a bit of tweaking, masking, and blending, you'll be able to achieve the perfect outcome every time.</p>
