<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="heading-level-4">Download and open the sample file</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open the supplied sample file, business-card-design_START.indd. Our sample business card uses standard US dimensions: 3.5 x 2 in. For Europe and elsewhere, use 55 mm x 85 mm. You can see the document settings in the Properties panel on the right, with nothing in the document selected.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You’re viewing the front of the business card in Normal mode, which lets you see non-printing objects like margins and text frames. Press the W key to switch between Normal and Preview modes — the Preview mode hides the distracting non-printing objects.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Press the W key to return to the Normal mode.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://helpx.adobe.com/content/dam/help/en/indesign/how-to/business-card-design/jcr_content/main-pars/image/business-card-design-step1_900x506.jpg" alt="Cards"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="heading-level-4">Place a logo on the front of the card.</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose File &gt; Place and navigate to the supplied logo-color.ai file. Click Open.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With your cursor loaded with the logo art, click at the intersection of the top and left margins and drag a rectangular frame.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To resize the logo and frame proportionally, with the Selection tool selected in the Toolbar, press Command+Shift (macOS) or Control+Shift (Windows) and drag a corner point.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you need to move the logo, click the frame with the Selection tool and drag it to the desired position on the page.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://helpx.adobe.com/content/dam/help/en/indesign/how-to/business-card-design/jcr_content/main-pars/image_1802581711/business-card-design-step2_900x506.jpg" alt="Cards"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="heading-level-4">Replace placeholder text</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the Type tool in the Toolbar.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Highlight each line of text individually and replace it with your own text. These steps maintain the font, text layout, and text styles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://helpx.adobe.com/content/dam/help/en/indesign/how-to/business-card-design/jcr_content/main-pars/image_93227648/business-card-design-step3_900x506.jpg" alt="Cards"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":6} -->
<h6 class="wp-block-heading" id="heading-level-6">4</h6>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="heading-level-4">Style your text</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Although the text is already formatted, you can customize it by changing various settings in the Character section of the Properties panel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the text frame with the Selection tool to format an entire text frame.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click the down-facing arrow next to the font name and select a font installed on your computer, or click Find More to find and activate an Adobe Font.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://helpx.adobe.com/content/dam/help/en/indesign/how-to/business-card-design/jcr_content/main-pars/image_1733030550/business-card-design-step41900x506.jpg" alt="Cards"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To format some text differently, select it with the Type tool and try changing up the look.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Select your name, and then click the All Caps button in the Properties panel. Increase the font size to make it stand out.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Select your title and practice changing the font and font style.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://helpx.adobe.com/content/dam/help/en/indesign/how-to/business-card-design/jcr_content/main-pars/image_1963047609/business-card-design-step42900x506.jpg" alt="Cards"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="heading-level-4">Design the back of the card</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now, you’ll finish the back of the card. Click the tab for the Pages panel or choose Window &gt; Pages. Double-click the page 2 thumbnail to show it in the document window.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the black rectangle with the Selection tool and click the Fill Color box under Appearance in the Properties panel. Select a color swatch — you may have to scroll to see all the colors we provided.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://helpx.adobe.com/content/dam/help/en/indesign/how-to/business-card-design/jcr_content/main-pars/image_296720916/business-card-design-step5a_900x506.jpg" alt="Cards"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next, you’ll place another logo using what you learned earlier.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose File &gt; Place and navigate to the supplied logo-white.ai file. Click Open.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the loaded cursor, drag a rectangular frame somewhere on the colored rectangle. Press Command+Shift (macOS) or Control+Shift and drag a corner point on the frame to resize proportionally.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now, you'll position the logo.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Drag the logo to the center until you see intersecting Smart Guides that help you align your logo precisely.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="https://helpx.adobe.com/content/dam/help/en/indesign/how-to/business-card-design/jcr_content/main-pars/image_873258876/business-card-design-step5b_900x506.jpg" alt="Cards"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":6} -->
<h6 class="wp-block-heading" id="heading-level-6">6</h6>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="heading-level-4">Save your business card</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose File &gt; Export &gt; Adobe PDF (Print) and click Save.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the Adobe PDF Preset menu, change the following:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose either the High Quality Print or Press Quality options, based on your print provider's recommendations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the Marks and Bleed Marks category and:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Select Crop Marks and Bleed Marks in the Marks section.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Select Use Document Bleed Settings in the Bleed and Slug section.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click Export to save a print-ready PDF that you can email or upload online.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Note:</strong>&nbsp;Since the background runs to the edge of the paper, the sample file is saved with a bleed. The bleed extends the design slightly beyond the page edges so that no white edges appear when the cards are printed and trimmed. See How to Set a Print Bleed to learn more.</p>
