<h2>STYLE STYLES</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Brand Review: Lecture. You can view&nbsp;the presentation&nbsp;<a href="https://www.dropbox.com/s/9qpv9x77udbhdoj/4_The_Design_process_4_365890_creative_brief.pptx?dl=0">here</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Style Tiles are a design deliverable consisting of fonts, colors and interface elements that communicate the essence of a visual brand for the web.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>They help form a common visual language between the designers and the stakeholders and provide a catalyst for discussions around the preferences and goals of the client.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Style Tiles are similar to the paint chips and fabric swatches an interior designer gets approval on before designing a room. An interior designer doesn't design three different rooms for a client at the first kick-off meeting, so why do Web designers design three different webpage mockups?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote {"className":"is-style-large"} -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-style-large"><p>Present clients with interface choices without making the investment in&nbsp;multiple photoshop&nbsp;mockups.</p></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>WHEN TO USE STYLE&nbsp;TILES?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Style tiles are for when a moodboard is too vague and a comp is too literal. Style tiles establish a direct connection with actual interface elements without defining layout. They work well for clients who have established brands and need them to translate smoothly to the web. Whereas the word&nbsp;<strong>“mood”</strong>&nbsp;is often associated with brand and identity design, the word&nbsp;<strong>“style”</strong>&nbsp;was chosen to mirror&nbsp;<strong>“cascading stylesheets”</strong>&nbsp;and reinforce that Style Tiles are specific to Web design.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is the actual process of composing the style tile. Determine themes from the aggregated adjectives and begin to match them up with&nbsp;styles. The Style Tiles give the client a point of reference to determine if you both are on the same page.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Elements of design</strong><br>Line<br>Color<br>Shape<br>Form<br>Pattern<br>Space<br>Texture</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Principles of design</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unity<br>Harmony<br>Balance<br>Rhythm<br>Emphasis</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Part 1. Please complete the following.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li>Determine a color palette.</li><li>Select a limited range of shapes, textures, and patterns for your strategy.</li><li>Find or create imagery or fine art that inspires you. Some people include industrial design images that inspire them.</li><li>Select one or two typefaces.</li></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Please organize your assets (You can consider the above list as <strong>"asset collection"</strong> for your Style Tiles) on an 11" x 17" Artboard in Illustrator. As always you may deviate from the above dimensions if your concept warrents it. Please ask for my advice regarding this.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3><strong>Part 2</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"align":"left"} -->
<p class="has-text-align-left">Please include the following in addition to what we reviewed in class the following info.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li>All color swatches and Hex/RGB values</li><li>Various textures and patterns</li><li>Icons for usability social media icons.</li><li>Please make sure the style of these icons is inspired by your brand.</li><li>Headers: Color, Font and size</li><li>Sub-Headers: Color, Font and size</li><li>Body copy: Color, Font and size image samples and how they may be treated your Logo</li><li>Adjective cloud</li><li>Overall Background colors.</li><li>Please use these in your style tile</li><li>Button and Link treatments</li><li>Examples of layout sections.</li><li>How is information displayed throughout your website and social media?</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These are just a few. Be creative. Have fun. Include as much information as you can. Remember this isn’t just an informational document. It is also a composition.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Be creative.</strong> Break the template if needed. (Just be neat). Please show me drastic layout ideas by me for approval.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your board should establish a visual position in line with the brand strategy and provide a design direction.</p>
