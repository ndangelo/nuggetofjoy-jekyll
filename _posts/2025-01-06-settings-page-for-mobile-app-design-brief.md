<p><strong>Design Scenario</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As a UI/UX designer, you're tasked with improving the settings page of a mobile app after receiving numerous user reports indicating that the current settings page is unclear. The page contains many options, potentially causing cognitive overload. Your goal is to design a settings page that is user-friendly and helps people easily find what they need without feeling overwhelmed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Design Task</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your design task is to complete the following:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Select the industry and platform for your design.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Conduct research to understand user needs and main friction points.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Design a settings page for mobile.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Provide an explanation of your design decisions.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Bonus points for making your project submission interactive.🏆</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Evaluation criteria</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your project will be evaluated using the following criteria:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Scannability</strong>&nbsp;— How easily and quickly users can scan or glance through a digital interface to find the information they need without having to read every word?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Usability</strong>&nbsp;— Can users perform tasks safely, effectively, and efficiently while enjoying the experience?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Visual design</strong>&nbsp;— Do designs effectively engage the user through strategic use of color, typography, and imagery?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Presentation</strong>&nbsp;— How effectively is the work presented, including the templates provided, the formatting of the copy, the links shared, and the explanation of the design rationale?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
