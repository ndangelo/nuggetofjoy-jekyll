<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">PROJECT BRIEF</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><span style="box-sizing: border-box; margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">Use&nbsp;<a href="https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/topics/systems-thinking#:~:text=Systems%20thinking%20is%20an%20approach,and%20avoid%20merely%20treating%20symptoms." target="_blank">the systems thinking process</a>&nbsp;initially proposed in the book Design to Renourish and any design methods (cultural probes, storytelling, user flows/personas, life cycle analysis, etc.)&nbsp; that make sense with your chosen topic and strategies.</span> Document everything. Create a research database for the team you will share throughout the project.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Pick one of these general larger issues below and propose a specific sub-topic. Go deep into the problem following the Re-nourish systems thinking process to define and create a design solution that ideally is carbon positive. It is best to keep the sub-topic more local or regional. Small bets! The project will be critiqued in a public setting.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Health<br></strong><em>(e.g.) How might we make the Emergency Department waiting room more humane and user-focused?</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Education</strong><br><em>(e.g.) How might we improve education and expand learning opportunities for refugees worldwide?</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Democracy</strong><br><em>(e.g.) How might we design an accessible election experience for everyone? How can we more engage people to vote?</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Planet</strong><br><em>(e.g.) How can we effectively change our consumption habits, keep a high quality of life, and stop global warming?</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>---</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-pale-cyan-blue-background-color has-background" style="font-size:32px">The Goals of Systems Thinking&nbsp;</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Eliminate waste</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Renourish our planet (reparations for nature)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Renourish our souls with a beautiful object or service</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Create reciprocity (a gift that creates an ongoing relationship)&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Create with, not for – inclusivity&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Have a purpose, not just profit (improve quality of life)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Advocacy for marginalized voices (reparations for others)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">LEARNING OBJECTIVES</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3,"backgroundColor":"pale-cyan-blue","fontSize":"medium"} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-pale-cyan-blue-background-color has-background has-medium-font-size"><strong>Students will learn during this project:</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Sustainable Design Strategies</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Human Centered Design principles</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">New design methods and tools</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">The "Design to Renourish" systems thinking method:<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>1. Determine project goals</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>2. Map out the design problem</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>3. Brainstorm design outcomes</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>4. Evaluate each possible project outcome</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">DELIVERABLES</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>At the end of the project, students should deliver:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">A digital presentation that details the project goals&nbsp;completed in Step Two from the "Design to Renourish" systems thinking method</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">A detailed and visually stimulating digital mapping of the design problem completed in Step Two from the "Design to Renourish" systems thinking method</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">A digital presentation of possible design solutions and the process/methods/tools used to determine which project was the best considering the audience(s) and the planet</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Your final solution implemented in the format and medium that best fits the design problem, audience(s), and planet</li>
