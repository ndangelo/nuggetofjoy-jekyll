<p class="single-first-p"><strong>Typography&nbsp;</strong>is the art of arranging, designing and modifying type.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<div class="OBXcSRpc">
<div class="wix-medium"><span style="font-family: Lato; font-size: inherit;">Creating a well designed typography poster is no easy task, and takes time and patience.</span></div>
</div>
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When a poster focuses only on typography as its main element, the designer has to carefully craft his design to make sure that the type is&nbsp;<strong>both legible and artistic</strong>&nbsp;at the same time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
