<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">PROJECT BRIEF</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Select an entry from&nbsp;<a href="https://peoplesgdarchive.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">The People's Graphic Design Archive</a>. Write an informative but brief history about it for the&nbsp;<a href="https://www.instagram.com/psugd_designhistory" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">PSUGD Design History Instagram</a>&nbsp;that connects it to a <strong>current issue, designer, or concept.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong><a href="https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/ojjx7gs20pqdzygt0hw04/Instagram-Post-Mockup.zip?rlkey=ujbqdadej61l9vkee7pnhojyp&amp;dl=1" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">INSTAGRAM TEMPLATE</a></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Include</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Some&nbsp;<strong>History</strong>&nbsp;of the piece.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Make sure you integrate&nbsp;<strong>context</strong>&nbsp;for that piece. It could include why the designer/producer worked in a certain way (regional, client, politics, social atmosphere, etc.).&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Explain&nbsp;<strong>why</strong>&nbsp;it is important as a piece of graphic design and how it relates to an issue today. This is the meat of the piece. It's the&nbsp;<strong>analysis</strong>&nbsp;of the work and its relevance that makes it a critical reading, rather than just a summary.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Specs</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>2,200 characters&nbsp;</strong>(roughly 380 words, per IG max)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>3 reliable sources</strong>&nbsp;required (though this will not be included in the IG post).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>10 JPGS</strong>&nbsp;(minimum) of the topic</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"backgroundColor":"pale-cyan-blue"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-pale-cyan-blue-background-color has-background">LEARNING OBJECTIVES</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Evolve critical writing skills</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Learn to make connections between the past and present</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Learn about graphic design histories outside of the canon</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Learn to work with a digital archive</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">DELIVERABLES</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Please submit text with images to be uploaded to a class Instagram account. (Dropbox) (3 citations are required, but are not posted on Instagram due to limited space.)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
