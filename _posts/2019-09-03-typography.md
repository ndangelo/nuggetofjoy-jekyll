<h3>Signature Logo</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li> With the notion that your “handwriting” is you, your DNA so to speak, start by writing your name. Determine if your signature has any characteristics that might characterize your personality. Hand make, hand draw, or hand letter the letterforms of your name, retaining any quirks or imperfections that might just be “you.”</li></ol>
