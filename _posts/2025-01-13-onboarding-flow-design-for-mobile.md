<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Design Scenario</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">As an interaction designer, you’ve been tasked with designing a mobile onboarding flow for an app of your choosing. Your goal is to create an intuitive, quick, but meaningful flow that follows the best practices of mobile design.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Design Task</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Your design task is to complete the following:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Select an industry for a mobile app for your design.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Identify and analyze users' main friction points when entering the onboarding flow.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Design an onboarding flow.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Provide a detailed explanation of your design decisions.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Use the provided mobile templates to showcase your designs.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Bonus points for making your project submission interactive. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Your project will be evaluated using the following criteria:</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Friction Points 💥</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Complicated Navigation</strong>: If there are too many steps or the setup is confusing, users might find it hard to get started.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Too Much Information</strong>: Showing too many features or details at once can overwhelm users. Keeping it simple can help hold their attention.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Unclear Purpose</strong>: If it’s not obvious why the app is useful, users may lose interest and stop using it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Design Rationale 💡</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">The onboarding design is simple and elegant. It warmly welcomes users and immediately shows the app's value. The screens, with clean colors and easy-to-read text, guide users through just a few quick steps, making the first experience with the app easy and visually appealing.</p>
