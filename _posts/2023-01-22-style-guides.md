<h2>What you need to include. </h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the very least, you need to include these three things on your sheet: </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Your logo.&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Color palette with Hex numbers.&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Primary and secondary fonts.&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>Why you need a brand style guide.&nbsp;</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Regardless of whether you’re a one-woman shop or have a team and are growing every day, it’s a good idea to have a brand style guide. Below are four reasons it will make your life easier and ensure you stay on-brand as you grow: </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Make sure your branding elements look good together. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Make sure team members use the right fonts and colors, so your branding stays consistent. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Ensure outside contractors (videographers, designers, etc.) get your look and vibe, so you don’t miss the mark when creating new material. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Use internally as a guide when developing new programs or products to make sure all of your offerings look cohesive&nbsp;so you can continue to build brand recognition.&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As you can see, it may take a few minutes to put your brand style guide together, but it will save you time and headaches as you grow your business. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
