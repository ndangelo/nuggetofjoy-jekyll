<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Design Scenario</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">You're a member of the design team at a video streaming service. Your task is to brainstorm ideas and design wireframes that offer a unique approach to service usability, setting it apart from competitors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Design Task</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Your design task is to complete the following:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Select a video streaming service and the device type for your design.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Brainstorm ideas on how to enhance a video streaming service.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Develop interactive wireframes for the most compelling ideas, choosing the level of fidelity you prefer.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Explain the rationale behind your wireframes.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Use the provided mobile and desktop templates to showcase your designs.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Bonus points for conducting a competitive analysis, identifying the strengths and weaknesses of competitors. 🏆</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Evaluation criteria</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your project will be evaluated using the following criteria:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Usability</strong> — Can users perform tasks safely, effectively, and efficiently while enjoying the experience?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Creativity</strong> — Does it solve problems and communicate ideas in an innovative way?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Feasibility</strong> — How practical and realistic a proposed design solution is within the potential constraints of a project?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Visual design</strong> — Do designs effectively engage the user through strategic use of color, typography, and imagery?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Presentation</strong> — How effectively is the work presented, including the templates provided, the formatting of the copy, the links shared, and the explanation of the design rationale?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
