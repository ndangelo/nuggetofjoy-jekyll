<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Develop your skills in creating a color system that resonates with your audience and effectively communicates the product's message.</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Design Scenario</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You are a UI designer for a digital work management tool. Your team is reimagining the brand image, aiming to create a fresher look that sets the tool apart from competitors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Design Task</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your design task is to complete the following:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Select a work management platform and the device type for your design.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Display primary, secondary, tertiary, neutral, and system colors in your color system.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Outline brand principles reflected by the color system.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Detail emotions targeted by the colors.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Confirm WCAG color contrast compliance.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Show color usage in branding and UI elements.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Use the provided color system templates to showcase your work.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Offer a comprehensive explanation of your design decisions.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Instructions</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Read the instructions to ensure your submission receives maximum visibility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>You can edit your project at any time.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>After submitting your project, consider reviewing others' projects. This can enhance your project's discoverability.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Ensure you only submit your own work.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Evaluation criteria</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your project will be evaluated using the following criteria:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Visual design</strong>&nbsp;— Do designs effectively engage the user through strategic use of color, typography, and imagery?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Accessibility</strong>&nbsp;— Does the design comply with the WCAG’s requirements and is accessible to all users? Is the design inclusive to all users regardless of their background, race, age, gender, and ethnicity?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Presentation</strong>&nbsp;— How effectively is the work presented, including the templates provided, the formatting of the copy, the links shared, and the explanation of the design rationale?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
