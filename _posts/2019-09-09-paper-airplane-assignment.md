<p><strong>Paper Airplane Assignment:&nbsp;&nbsp;</strong>Fold a paper airplane, place it in front of you &amp; draw from direct observation as accurately as you can.&nbsp;ADD FULL SHADING <strong><em>Possible Medium:&nbsp;</em></strong>drawing pencil </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/1454543173311-14.jpeg" alt="Paper Airplane DIY"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sam Henderson</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For best results use a flat, thin-edged ruler. Make good creases along each edge. Adding double stick tape to the inside of the body keeps the wings from separating in flight.</p>

<p><strong>The Steath</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This one is built for distance, plus it just looks cool.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/14545432045324.jpeg" alt="Paper Airplane DIY"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/14545432155674.jpeg" alt="Paper Airplane DIY"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/14545432158224.jpeg" alt="Paper Airplane DIY"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li>Fold the paper in half vertically.</li><li>Unfold the paper and fold each of the top corners into the center line.</li><li>Fold the peak toward you and 3/4” from the bottom of the paper.</li><li>Fold both top corners into the center line.</li><li>Fold the remaining tip over the two flaps at the center line to lock them in place.</li><li>Fold the plane in half away from you.</li><li>Fold the wings down 1” from the bottom of the plane.</li><li>Fold up the sides of each wing 1/2” tall.</li><li>Cut two small slits at the back of each wing. Fold up the tabs.</li><li>Add double stick tape to the inside of the body. The finished plane should look like this.</li></ol>

<p><strong>The Bumble</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Built correctly, this bee-like beauty stays in the air for a long time.</p>

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/14545431620104.jpeg" alt="Paper Airplane DIY"/></figure>

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li>Fold the paper in half horizontally.</li><li>Unfold the paper and fold each of the top corners into the center line.</li><li>Fold the peak down to meet the edge of the previous fold.</li><li>Fold the upper sides into the center line.</li><li>Fold the top edge 1/2” away from you.</li><li>Fold the plane in half towards you.</li><li>Fold the wings down 1/2” from the bottom of the plane.</li><li>Add double stick tape to the inside of the body. The finished plane should look like this.</li></ol>

<p><strong>The Hunter</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Count on speed and distance from this sleek plane.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/14545431589194.jpeg" alt="Paper Airplane DIY"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/14545432032544.jpeg" alt="Paper Airplane DIY"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li>Fold the paper in half vertically.</li><li>Unfold the paper and fold the top edge down 2”.</li><li>Fold the top edge down again to meet the bottom of the previous fold.</li><li>Fold the top edge down one more time to meet the bottom of the previous fold.</li><li>Fold the top edges down and away from you to the center line on the back of the paper.</li><li>Fold the plane in half towards you.</li><li>Starting at the top of the thick nose of the plane, fold the wings down 1/2” in the front and slightly angled to 1 1/2” in the back. Fold up the edges of the wings 1/2”.</li><li>Add double stick tape to the inside of the body. The finished plane should look like this.</li></ol>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>The Bullnose</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This plane is deceptively fast and stays aloft for quite a distance.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/14545432226674.jpeg" alt="Paper Airplane DIY"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/14545432308184.jpeg" alt="Paper Airplane DIY"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li>Fold the top right corner over to the left side, making a fold from the top of the paper to the bottom right corner.</li><li>Unfold the paper and repeat for the left corner.</li><li>Unfold the paper again and fold the top right corner over to meet the crease made from the first fold.</li><li>Fold the left corner over to meet the crease from the second fold.</li><li>Fold the top right edge over to meet the edge of the fold from step 3. Repeat for the other side.</li><li>Fold the top edge down and towards you to meet the point where the right and left layers cross.</li><li>Fold the plane in half away from you. Staring at the nose, fold the wings down at a slight angle, 1/2” in the front to about 2” in the back.</li><li>Add double stick tape to the inside of the body. The finished plane should look like this.</li></ol>
