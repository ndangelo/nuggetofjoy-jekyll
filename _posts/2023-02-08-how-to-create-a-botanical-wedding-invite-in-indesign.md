<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>1. How to Set Up Your Invite Document in InDesign</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open InDesign and click on<strong>&nbsp;Create New (or go to File &gt; New &gt; Document).</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click on&nbsp;<strong>Print</strong>&nbsp;at the top of the&nbsp;<strong>New Document window</strong>. Switch the&nbsp;<strong>Units</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Inches,</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Width</strong>&nbsp;of the page to 5 in and&nbsp;<strong>Height</strong>&nbsp;to 7 in.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8345} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-12.55.14-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign create new" class="wp-image-8345"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Add a&nbsp;<strong>Bleed</strong>&nbsp;of 0.125 in, and&nbsp;<strong>click Create.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Expand the <strong>Layers panel</strong> <strong>(Window > Layers)</strong> and double-click on the <strong>Layer 1</strong> name. Rename it as <strong>Background</strong> and <strong>click OK</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose&nbsp;<strong>New Layer</strong>&nbsp;from the panel’s top-right drop-down menu. Name this layer&nbsp;<strong>Leaves</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>click OK</strong>. Create two more layers—first,&nbsp;<strong>Type Border</strong>, and, finally,&nbsp;<strong>Type</strong>&nbsp;at the top of the sequence.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8346} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-12.56.05-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign layers panel" class="wp-image-8346"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock all layers except&nbsp;<strong>Background,</strong>&nbsp;which we’ll work on first.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8347} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-12.56.37-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign new swatch" class="wp-image-8347"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Expand the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches panel (Window &gt; Color &gt; Swatches)</strong>&nbsp;and choose<strong>&nbsp;New Color Swatch</strong>&nbsp;from the panel’s menu.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose <strong>New Color Swatch</strong> from the panel’s menu. Name the swatch <strong>Off-White,</strong> and with the <strong>Type</strong> set to <strong>Process</strong> and <strong>Mode</strong> to CMYK, set the levels below to C=4 M=3 Y=5 K=0. Then <strong>click Add and Done.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8348} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-12.57.21-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign new swatch" class="wp-image-8348"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a second new swatch, naming it&nbsp;<strong>Peach</strong>&nbsp;and setting the levels to C=6 M=41 Y=37 K=0.<strong></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>2. How to Build Up Color on Your Invite</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Working on the&nbsp;<strong>Background layer,</strong>&nbsp;take the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangle Tool (M)</strong>&nbsp;and drag across the page, extending the shape up to the edge of the bleed on all sides.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8349} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-12.58.11-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign new document" class="wp-image-8349"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches panel</strong>&nbsp;set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill&nbsp;</strong>of the shape to<strong>&nbsp;Off-White.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock the&nbsp;<strong>Background layer</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>unlock the Type Border layer.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangle Tool (M)</strong>&nbsp;to create a second shape over the top of the first, also with an&nbsp;<strong>Off-White Fill.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8350} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-12.59.38-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign rectangle tool" class="wp-image-8350"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Make this shape smaller, 3.5 in in&nbsp;<strong>Width</strong>&nbsp;and 4.5 in in&nbsp;<strong>Height,</strong>&nbsp;and position centrally on the page.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With this smaller rectangle selected,&nbsp;<strong>go to Object &gt; Effects &gt; Drop Shadow.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click on the colored square to the right of the&nbsp;<strong>Mode menu,</strong>&nbsp;and switch the&nbsp;<strong>Effect Color to Peach,</strong>&nbsp;before clicking&nbsp;<strong>OK.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8351} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.00.37-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign effect color" class="wp-image-8351"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bring the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;of the shadow down to around 25%, increase the&nbsp;<strong>Size</strong>&nbsp;to 0.625 in and add about 10%&nbsp;<strong>Noise.</strong>&nbsp;Then&nbsp;<strong>click OK</strong>&nbsp;to exit the window.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8352} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.01.14-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign black shape" class="wp-image-8352"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create another rectangle shape over the top of this one, making it slightly smaller and leaving a small margin between the two.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches panel</strong>&nbsp;set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;of this shape to [None] and the&nbsp;<strong>Stroke Color</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Peach.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8353} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.02.12-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign rectangle border" class="wp-image-8353"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From the&nbsp;<strong>Stroke panel (Window &gt; Stroke)</strong>, increase the&nbsp;<strong>Weight</strong>&nbsp;of the shape’s edge to 2.25 pt.<strong></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>3. How to Format Typography on Your Invite</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock the&nbsp;<strong>Type Border layer</strong>&nbsp;and unlock the top layer,&nbsp;<strong>Type.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Switch to the&nbsp;<strong>Type Tool (T)</strong>&nbsp;and drag across the border shapes, creating a text frame about a third of the way down.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8354} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.03.01-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign type tool" class="wp-image-8354"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Type in&nbsp;<strong>‘Name 1’,</strong>&nbsp;and from either the top&nbsp;<strong>Controls panel</strong>&nbsp;or the&nbsp;<strong>Character</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Paragraph panels</strong>&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Type &amp; Tables &gt; Character/Paragraph)</strong>, set the&nbsp;<strong>Font to Sen, Size 19 pt, Tracking (letter-spacing) to 300, All Caps and Align Center.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8355} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.03.33-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign type tool" class="wp-image-8355"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Copy</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Paste</strong>&nbsp;the text frame, to create a copy below, editing the text to read ‘Name 2’.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create another text frame between the two names, typing in an ampersand, ‘&amp;’, and setting the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Cormorant Garamond, SemiBold Italic, Size 18 pt, Align Center.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8356} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.04.02-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign type tool ampersand" class="wp-image-8356"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches panel</strong>&nbsp;adjust the&nbsp;<strong>Font Color to Peach.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a text frame above the names, typing in intro text like ‘join us to celebrate / the union of’.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8357} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.04.23-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign type tool" class="wp-image-8357"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Cormorant Garamond Regular, Size 7 pt, Leading (line-spacing) to 15 pt, Tracking 300 and Align Center.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Copy</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Paste</strong>&nbsp;the text frame a couple of times, positioning these below the names and editing the text to read the date, time and location of the event.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8358} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.04.42-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign type tool" class="wp-image-8358"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set another text frame with ‘reception to follow’ at the bottom, set in&nbsp;<strong>Cormorant Garamond Italic, Size 8 pt, Tracking 200 and Align Center.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can divide up sections of information by setting a text frame between them and setting the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Cormorant Garamond Regular and Font Color to Peach.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8359} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.06.00-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign glyphs panel" class="wp-image-8359"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8360} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.06.27-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign layers panel" class="wp-image-8360"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Go to Window &gt; Type &amp; Tables &gt; Glyphs.</strong>&nbsp;Set your&nbsp;<strong>Type Tool cursor</strong>&nbsp;in the text frame, and identify a bullet glyph from the list of glyphs in the panel. Double-click to insert it into the frame.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>4. How to Add a Botanical Touch to Your Invite</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8361} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.07.11-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign olive wreath" class="wp-image-8361"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open up the olive wreath vector illustration in&nbsp;<strong>Adobe Illustrator,</strong>&nbsp;or other vector software of your choice.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8362} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.07.24-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign olive vector" class="wp-image-8362"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Isolate individual leaves and&nbsp;<strong>Copy</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Paste</strong>&nbsp;them into new documents.&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Save As</strong>&nbsp;each as it’s own<strong>&nbsp;Illustrator or EPS file</strong>, with a transparent background.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Head back to your InDesign document.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock the&nbsp;<strong>Type layer</strong>&nbsp;and unlock the&nbsp;<strong>Leaves layer</strong>&nbsp;below. Create a small image frame using the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangle Frame Tool (F),</strong>&nbsp;over the top-right corner of the type border.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8363} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.07.51-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign olive vector" class="wp-image-8363"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>File &gt; Place,</strong>&nbsp;choosing one of your individual leaf images and&nbsp;<strong>clicking Open.</strong>&nbsp;Allow it to fill the frame completely, and scale and rotate to allow it to frame the corner of the rectangle shape nicely.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8364} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.08.10-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign olive vector" class="wp-image-8364"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Repeat the process, creating more individual image frames over both the top-right and bottom-left corners of the type border, and placing individual leaf images into each.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>5. How to Export Your Finished Invite for Printing</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Go to File &gt; Export</strong>, and choose&nbsp;<strong>Adobe PDF (Print)</strong>&nbsp;from the&nbsp;<strong>Format menu</strong>&nbsp;at the bottom of the&nbsp;<strong>Export window.</strong>&nbsp;Name the file and hit&nbsp;<strong>Save.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8365} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.08.48-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign export pdf" class="wp-image-8365"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the Export Adobe PDF window that opens, choose [Press Quality] from the&nbsp;<strong>Adobe PDF Preset menu</strong>&nbsp;at the top.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:nextpage -->
<!--nextpage-->
<!-- /wp:nextpage -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click on&nbsp;<strong>Marks and Bleeds</strong>&nbsp;in the window’s left-hand menu.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Some print services will prefer you to either include printer’s marks and a bleed, or only to include the bleed, so make sure to check your printer’s preferences beforehand.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8366} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.08.58-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign marks and bleeds" class="wp-image-8366"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here, I’ve checked both&nbsp;<strong>All Printer’s Marks</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Use Document Bleed Settings</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Click Export</strong>&nbsp;to create your print-ready file.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","id":8367} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen-Shot-2018-05-25-at-13.09.23-4.jpg" alt="botanical rustic wedding invite indesign press ready pdf" class="wp-image-8367"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And you’re done! Great job! You can send this PDF file straight off for printing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
