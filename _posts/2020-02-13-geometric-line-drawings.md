<h2>Assignment</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>In your sketchbook complete the following:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li>Create a simple geometric shape using a ruler.</li><li>Create and build multiple shapes within it.</li><li>Create intersecting points.</li><li>When you have completed your finished drawings and they have been approved. Use Three colors to accent some of the shapes.</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before you start the above projects create small sketches first. Use two pages in your sketchbook. Quarter each sheet. Draw a sample drawing similar to the above directions.</p>
