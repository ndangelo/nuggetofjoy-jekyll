<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1.&nbsp;How to Create a Pride Rainbow Gradient in Photoshop</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To create a colorful gradient in Photoshop, first, locate the&nbsp;<strong>Layer Adjustments</strong>&nbsp;icon at the bottom of the&nbsp;<strong>Layers</strong>&nbsp;panel. It's an icon of a circle that's half filled in.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select and choose<strong>&nbsp;Gradient&nbsp;</strong>from the dropdown menu. This will create a&nbsp;<strong>Gradient Fill&nbsp;</strong>layer.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0006_Photoshop_xgUyaREcOO-3.jpg" alt="create a new gradient fill layer "/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next, select the&nbsp;<strong>Gradient</strong>&nbsp;window to open the<strong>&nbsp;Gradient Editor.</strong>&nbsp;A number of premade gradients will appear.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0005_Photoshop_xeOQ8mvP7N.jpg" alt="open the gradient editor "/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the<strong>&nbsp;Gradient</strong>&nbsp;settings box,&nbsp;<strong>double-click</strong>&nbsp;the bottom and leftmost anchor point, also called a&nbsp;<strong>Stop,&nbsp;</strong>to open the&nbsp;<strong>Color Picker.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The rainbow pride colors are:&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Red&nbsp;<code>#e40303</code>&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Orange&nbsp;<code>#ff8c00</code></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Yellow&nbsp;<code>#ffed00</code></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Green&nbsp;<code>#008026</code></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Blue&nbsp;<code>#004dff</code>&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Purple&nbsp;<code>#750787</code></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The leftmost&nbsp;<strong>Stop</strong>&nbsp;is the beginning of the gradient, so let's set it to Red&nbsp;<code>#e40303</code>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click&nbsp;<strong>OK.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0004_Photoshop_wFsjuBUeVD.jpg" alt="add in red color stop "/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now, select the rightmost<strong>&nbsp;Stop</strong>, setting it to a Purple&nbsp;<code>#750787</code>&nbsp;color.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If the&nbsp;<strong>Stop</strong>&nbsp;started out as transparent, select the upper left&nbsp;<strong>Stop</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity&nbsp;</strong>to&nbsp;<strong>100%</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0003_Photoshop_LtpKBjvAgZ.jpg" alt="add in purple color stop "/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click right below the&nbsp;<strong>Gradient</strong>&nbsp;box to create a new&nbsp;<strong>Stop</strong>. We want to create four new&nbsp;<strong>Stops</strong>&nbsp;between the original two. Try to make them evenly spaced by clicking the<strong>&nbsp;Stop&nbsp;</strong>and dragging.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0002_Photoshop_i1YvezNRSe.jpg" alt="addin four more stops"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 6</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Change the colors of the middle&nbsp;<strong>Stops</strong>&nbsp;to:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Orange&nbsp;<code>#ff8c00</code></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Yellow&nbsp;<code>#ffed00</code></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Green&nbsp;<code>#008026</code></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Blue&nbsp;<code>#004dff</code>&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can adjust the&nbsp;<strong>Stops&nbsp;</strong>to further refine your gradient. Click&nbsp;<strong>New&nbsp;</strong>to save your gradient as a Photoshop preset.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0001_Photoshop_tBUojb3lvr.jpg" alt="add in pride rainbow colors "/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/final_pride.jpg" alt="pride rainbow gradient "/></figure>

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2.&nbsp;How to Create a Rainbow Photoshop Effect</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select one of the rainbow gradient backgrounds we created above; I'll be using the Pride rainbow gradient.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose the gradient and press&nbsp;<strong>OK.&nbsp;&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Adjust the&nbsp;<strong>Style, Angle,</strong>&nbsp;and<strong>&nbsp;Scale</strong>&nbsp;to fit your photo. My settings can be seen below, but they will depend on the image you are using and what color you'd like to appear most dominant.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Photoshop_zl9V09Pfr9.jpg" alt="pride rainbow gradient settings "/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Finally. set the gradients' layer mode to&nbsp;<strong>Screen</strong>&nbsp;and a&nbsp;<strong>50% Opacity.&nbsp;</strong>This will create a beautiful rainbow overlay in Photoshop.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-cx2i-thats-it">That's It! You Know How to Make Colorful Gradients in Photoshop</h2>

<p>That is all there is to creating a rainbow gradient in Photoshop. Input as many colors as you like, or try playing with the different positions of the color Stops to get several different effects. And then you can save each gradient to create your very own custom set of rainbow gradient presets and rainbow overlays in Photoshop.</p>
