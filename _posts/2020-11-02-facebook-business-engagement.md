<h2><strong>Assignment: Social Media Campaign</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the following guidlines to create your own social media campaign for your business. Include the following in your campaign.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You will be creating a unified campaign that will span across <strong>Facebook, Instagram and Twitter. </strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It could be:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Scavenger hunt (with an incentive)</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Special Deal</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>NonProfit (specific event)</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>or more</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Users should be led from one social media format to another - and then perhaps a location? Who knows?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This campaign must include:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>1. Five Facebook posts.</strong> One with a professional-looking advertisement. (You can use Adobe CC or Spark.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>2. <strong>A formal video ad.</strong> (Spark is perfect for this)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>3. One <strong>picture post.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>4. Two text only posts.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>(text should be included with all posts.)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>5. Instagram: Professional Advertisement.</strong> (A variation of the one you did on Facebook?)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>6. Four other "behind the scene pictures." pictures</strong> of your business that people don't normally see.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Twitter:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>7. </strong>Variation of the <strong>Posts you included on Facebook</strong>. (But don't make them exactly the same. Change the messaging? The color palette?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Remember the following:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>1. Be true to your brand</strong>. Each of your campaigns reflects your brand identity and values.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>2. Stay focused</strong>. Each campaign has a single, simple message at its core.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>3. They’re trackable. </strong>Each campaign has a distinct hashtag or channel, which means the companies can measure key indicators of success. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>4. They’re unique to each platform.</strong> Broadcasting identical content across every platform is less effort for your social media team, but it looks sloppy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>5. They’re timely.</strong> Every campaign should be responsive to current events and activities </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>6. They’re emotional.</strong> Be memorable, people should make you feel something when you see them.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>7. Be original. </strong>You should never try to imitate someone else’s successful idea.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
