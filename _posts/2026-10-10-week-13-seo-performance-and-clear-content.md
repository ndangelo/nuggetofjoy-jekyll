<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key concepts</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Search engine optimization begins with useful content and clear structure. Descriptive page titles, meaningful headings, helpful link text, and concise page descriptions help people understand what a page is about.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Performance matters too. Large images can slow down a page, especially on mobile connections. Use appropriately sized assets and compression, avoid unnecessary effects, and test the page in realistic conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>No checklist can predict a search ranking. Search systems use many signals, and good optimization should improve the experience rather than encourage misleading content.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
