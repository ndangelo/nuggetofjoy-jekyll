<p>Looking to entice customers to view a property on your books? This stylish real estate flyer template has a versatile design which can be easily adapted to your own content, photos, and branding. Suitable for beginners to InDesign, this tutorial will take you through the steps of creating the flyer from scratch and setting up the design ready for printing.&nbsp;</p>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>1.&nbsp;How to Set Up the Flyer Layout in InDesign</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open up<strong>&nbsp;Adobe InDesign</strong>&nbsp;and head up to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; New &gt; Document.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the&nbsp;<strong>Intent</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Print</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Number of Pages</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>1</strong>, and uncheck&nbsp;<strong>Facing Pages</strong>&nbsp;to set up a simple one-page document.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the&nbsp;<strong>Width</strong>&nbsp;of the page to&nbsp;<strong>8.5 in (215.9 mm)&nbsp;</strong>and&nbsp;<strong>Height</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>11 in (279.4 mm)</strong>, which is a standard flyer size.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the&nbsp;<strong>Top Margin</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>15 mm, Bottom</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>18 mm</strong>, and&nbsp;<strong>Left</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Right Margins</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>14 mm.&nbsp;</strong>Add a&nbsp;<strong>Bleed</strong>&nbsp;of&nbsp;<strong>5 mm</strong>&nbsp;to all edges of the page, and click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>&nbsp;to create the document.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/1.15.jpg" alt="new document window"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:nextpage -->
<!--nextpage-->
<!-- /wp:nextpage -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Expand the&nbsp;<strong>Layers</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Layers)</strong>&nbsp;and double-click on the default&nbsp;<strong><em>Layer 1</em></strong>&nbsp;name in the panel to open the&nbsp;<strong>Layer Options</strong>&nbsp;window. Rename the layer&nbsp;<strong><em>Photo</em></strong>&nbsp;and click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click on the&nbsp;<strong>Create New Layer&nbsp;</strong>button at the bottom of the panel to create a second layer. Rename it&nbsp;<strong><em>Shapes</em></strong>. Create a third and final layer and rename this&nbsp;<strong><em>Typography</em></strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/1.25.jpg" alt="layers panel"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock both the top&nbsp;<strong><em>Typography</em></strong>&nbsp;layer and&nbsp;<strong><em>Photo</em></strong>&nbsp;layer at the bottom, leaving the&nbsp;<strong><em>Shapes</em></strong>&nbsp;layer unlocked and selected.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/1.2.1-3.jpg" alt="shapes layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<h2>2.&nbsp;How to Create Your Color Palette</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This flyer design will need a four-color palette, with a dark neutral base color, a light neutral color for text, and two variants, light and dark, of a punchier color.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To create your palette, expand the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Color &gt; Swatches)</strong>, and click on the&nbsp;<strong>New Swatch</strong>&nbsp;button at the bottom of the panel.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>First, let’s create our dark base color. Name the swatch&nbsp;<strong>Off-Black</strong>, and set the&nbsp;<strong>Type</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Process</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Mode</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>CMYK</strong>. Set the levels to&nbsp;<strong>C=90 M=84 Y=47 K=63</strong>, and click&nbsp;<strong>Add</strong>&nbsp;and then&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.17.jpg" alt="off-black swatch"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a further three new CMYK swatches using the same process.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here, I’ve created a pale swatch named&nbsp;<strong>Grey</strong>,&nbsp;<strong>C=10 M=7 Y=7 K=0</strong>, and two yellow swatches:&nbsp;<strong>C=3 M=34 Y=93 K=0 (‘Mustard’)</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>C=7 M=17 Y=93 K=0 (‘Light Yellow’)</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.26.jpg" alt="mustard"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2.2.14.jpg" alt="swatch options"/></figure>

<h2>3.&nbsp;How to Create a Geometric Design for Your Flyer</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Take the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangle Tool (M)</strong>&nbsp;and drag across the page, extending it up to the bleed on the top, left and bottom edges, and across to roughly the halfway point of the page. Set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;to your&nbsp;<strong>Off-Black</strong>&nbsp;swatch.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.15.jpg" alt="rectangle tool"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the<strong>&nbsp;Pen Tool (P)</strong>&nbsp;and use it to create a diagonal shape extending across the page, from the bottom-left corner to the top of the right side of the page. You want the ribbon to be reasonably thick, with perfectly parallel lines.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.25.jpg" alt="pen tool"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you’ve created the rough shape and want to tweak the anchor points, you can use the<strong>&nbsp;Direct Selection Tool (A)</strong>&nbsp;to select individual points and move them around using the arrow keys.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you’re happy with the shape, adjust the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;to<strong>&nbsp;Light Yellow.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.2.1-4.jpg" alt="light yellow"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:nextpage -->
<!--nextpage-->
<!-- /wp:nextpage -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Take the&nbsp;<strong>Scissors Tool (C)</strong>&nbsp;and snip once where the top of the yellow shape meets the edge of the black rectangle, and once again where the shapes cross over at the bottom. This will split the yellow shape into two sections.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.35.jpg" alt="scissors tool"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the top-right half of the yellow shape and go to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Effects &gt; Transparency.</strong>&nbsp;Choose&nbsp;<strong>Hard Light&nbsp;</strong>from the&nbsp;<strong>Mode</strong>&nbsp;menu, and pull down the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;to around&nbsp;<strong>90%</strong>. Click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.3.14.jpg" alt="hard light"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:nextpage -->
<!--nextpage-->
<!-- /wp:nextpage -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Take the&nbsp;<strong>Pen Tool (P)&nbsp;</strong>again and zoom into the top-right corner of the page. Trace the edges of the blank space that remains, creating a sort of triangle shape.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.45.jpg" alt="pen tool"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;of this new shape to&nbsp;<strong>Off-Black.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.4.1-4.jpg" alt="off-black "/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Edit &gt; Copy</strong>&nbsp;the triangle shape, and&nbsp;<strong>Edit &gt; Paste in Place.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.4.2-3.jpg" alt="paste in place"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Return to the&nbsp;<strong>Layers</strong>&nbsp;panel and click on the small arrow to the left of the&nbsp;<strong><em>Shapes</em></strong>&nbsp;layer’s name, to expand the layer. Select the top ‘polygon’ element...</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>... and drag it down into the&nbsp;<strong><em>Photo</em></strong>&nbsp;layer below. You’ll have to unlock this layer first to do so.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.4.3-3.jpg" alt="photo layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then, with the moved triangle shape still selected,&nbsp;<strong>Right-Click (Windows) or Control-Click (Mac) &gt; Content &gt; Graphic</strong>&nbsp;to turn the shape into an image frame. We’ll come back to this a bit later.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.4.4-3.jpg" alt="content graphic"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:nextpage -->
<!--nextpage-->
<!-- /wp:nextpage -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Photo</em></strong>&nbsp;layer and return to the&nbsp;<strong><em>Shapes</em></strong>&nbsp;layer above. Select the triangle shape that’s sitting on this layer, and go to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Effects &gt; Transparency</strong>. Reduce the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>40%</strong>, and click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/3.5-3.jpg" alt="transparency"/></figure>

<h2>4.&nbsp;How to Add Photos to Your Flyer Design</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Shapes</em></strong>&nbsp;layer and unlock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Photo</em></strong>&nbsp;layer below.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.17.jpg" alt="photo layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Take the&nbsp;<strong>Pen Tool (P)&nbsp;</strong>and create another new shape. This time, focus on the bottom-right section of the page, creating a shape that fills the bottom-right gap as well as the top-right half of the yellow rectangle sitting on the layer above, This will create a rectangle shape with a triangular top.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.1.1-3.jpg" alt="pen tool"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;of the shape to<strong>&nbsp;[None]</strong>&nbsp;and then&nbsp;<strong>Right-Click (Windows) or Control-Click (Mac) &gt; Content &gt; Graphic</strong>&nbsp;to transform the shape into an image frame.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/4.1.2-3.jpg" alt="content graphic"/></figure>

Arrange&nbsp;<a href="https://pixabay.com/en/building-sky-architecture-urban-1841299/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">the image</a>&nbsp;(holding down&nbsp;<strong>Shift</strong>&nbsp;while you scale it) until it fills the whole frame.&nbsp;</p>

<h2>5.&nbsp;Add Branding and Typography to the Flyer</h2>

<h2>6.&nbsp;How to Customize and Export Your Finished Flyer</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It’s simple to change the color and branding of your flyer. To adjust the color scheme, create a new pair of light and dark color swatches from the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel. Here I’ve created a pale apple green and a slightly darker counterpart.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then unlock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Shapes</em></strong>&nbsp;layer and select the top ribbon rectangles and large diagonal shape sections. You can adjust the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;color of these to give your flyer a fresh look.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
