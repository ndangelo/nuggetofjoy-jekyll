<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Purpose &amp; Scope</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As we advance into the network era, the emerging field of information architecture is becoming increasingly important and sophisticated.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This course provides a practical understanding of fundamental concepts and methods of value to anyone involved in user-centered design.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our working definitions of information architecture will include:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The combination of organization, labeling and navigation schemes within an information system.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The structural design of an information space to facilitate task completion and intuitive access to content.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The art and science of classifying web sites and intranets to help people find and manage information.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>A discipline and community of practice focused on bringing principles of design and architecture to the digital landscape.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The course also explores broader issues surrounding usability, findability, knowledge management and strategy. We will invite guest speakers to offer varied perspectives.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
