<h1 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background" id="b29c">Sketching out user flows.</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="10e6">A user flow is a sequence of steps a persona takes inside an app or on a website to reach a goal. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="e335">When we think about user experience, one possible classification is that there are 4 levels:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"className":""} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>user journey</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>user flow</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>layout</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>UI element</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="5b84">(Of course, it’s just one type of possible classification.) This UX Knowledge Piece Sketch is about 2 collaborative ideation activities that focus on sketching out user flows:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"className":""} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>thumbnail sketches</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>master flows.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"backgroundColor":"pale-cyan-blue"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-pale-cyan-blue-background-color has-background" id="058e">Thumbnail sketches</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="043d">These provide a high-level overview of a project (or a task at hand). The 2 main goals are:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"className":""} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>showing what the key parts or key messages are on each screen</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>indicating how the user can move forward towards her goal.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="623f">A thumbnail sketch is a quick rough sketch, it does not contain</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"className":""} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>individual detailed UI elements</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>transitions</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>content / microcopy details.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="349e">For instance, in case of a booking website, these might be 4 steps of a flow:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"className":""} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>search: when and where</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>search engine result page that contains the places that matches the query</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>place details page</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>booking form</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="f5d8">Due to the low-fidelity level, it does not explain how the search could work or what we should show on the place details page (and in what order).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="abc7">Of course, you can create thumbnail sketches on your own, but if you do it collaboratively, your team will be able to get a more complete high-level overview quite effectively.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="f4c3">One technique for cumulating your ideas is creating master flows.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"backgroundColor":"pale-cyan-blue"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-pale-cyan-blue-background-color has-background" id="c22f">Master flows</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="97dc">Creating master flows means working alone or together, then combining your quick thumbnail sketches to map out a—hopefully—more complete flow.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="4880">This is how it works:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>give 8 sticky notes to each team member (of course you can modify this number), the important thing is that everyone should get a different color</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>select a flow, the associated persona and one of her core needs, goals</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>set a timer (timeboxing is helpful in case of a collaborative team activity) — e.g. 8 minutes</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>the goal of the exercise is to quickly sketch out the main steps of the flow individually</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:list {"className":""} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>give 8 sticky notes to each team member (of course you can modify this number), the important thing is that everyone should get a different color</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>select a flow, the associated persona and one of her core needs, goals</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>set a timer (timeboxing is helpful in case of a collaborative team activity) — e.g. 8 minutes</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>the goal of the exercise is to quickly sketch out the main steps of the flow individually</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="2605">After the individual sketching, team members combine their flows:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"className":""} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>first, they search for the entry point</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>then they find the second step, the third step, finally the exit point</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="4399">As a result of this mapping process, they will see that:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"className":""} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>there are steps that contain only one sticky note — meaning that only one team member thought about it</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>there are steps that appear on each sticky note color (meaning that everyone included it)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p id="cf10">The whole point of the exercise is to serve as a conversation starter, e.g.:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"className":""} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Are there any missing points?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What are the key parts?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Should we combine some of the steps?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Did we discover any new directions?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
