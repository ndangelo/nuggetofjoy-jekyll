<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>1.&nbsp;How to Start Creating an Action</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>First, open the photo that you want to work with. To open your photo, go to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Open</strong>, choose your photo, and click&nbsp;<strong>Open</strong>. Now, before we get started, just check a couple of things:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Your photo should be in&nbsp;<strong>RGB Color</strong>&nbsp;mode,&nbsp;<strong>8 Bits/Channel</strong>. To check this, go to&nbsp;<strong>Image &gt; Mode</strong>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>For best results, your photo size should be&nbsp;<strong>2000–4000 px wide/high</strong>. To check this, go to<strong>&nbsp;Image &gt; Image Size</strong>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Your photo should be the&nbsp;<strong>Background</strong>&nbsp;layer. If it is not, go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; New &gt; Background from Layer</strong>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/checking-image-size-and-mode-4.jpg" alt="checking image size and mode" title="Image: https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/uploads/users/1437/posts/33671/image/checking-image-size-and-mode.jpg"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<h2>2.&nbsp;How to Select the Subject</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before I show you how to add glitter to pictures, first we need to prepare the subject. Choose the&nbsp;<strong>Quick Selection Tool</strong>&nbsp;<strong>(W)</strong>&nbsp;and select the background of the photo. Use the&nbsp;<strong>Shift-Alt&nbsp;</strong>buttons on your keyboard to add or subtract areas from the selection. After you've made a perfect selection, press&nbsp;<strong>Control-Shift-I</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to invert the selection.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/making-selection-2-4.jpg" alt="making selection" title="Image: https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/uploads/users/1437/posts/33671/image/making-selection-2.jpg"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now go to&nbsp;<strong>Select &gt; Modify &gt; Smooth</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Sample Radius</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>5 px</strong>. Next, go to&nbsp;<strong>Select &gt; Modify &gt; Contract</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;set&nbsp;<strong>Contract By</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>2 px</strong>. Finally, go to&nbsp;<strong>Select &gt; Modify &gt; Feather&nbsp;</strong>and set the&nbsp;<strong>Feather Radius</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>2&nbsp;px</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/modifying-selection-2-4.jpg" alt="modifying selection" title="Image: https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/uploads/users/1437/posts/33671/image/modifying-selection-2.jpg"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Press&nbsp;<strong>Control-J</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to create a new layer using the selection, and name this new layer&nbsp;<strong>Subject</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-layer-using-selection-4.jpg" alt="creating new layer using selection"/></figure>

<h2>3.&nbsp;How to Create the Background</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In this section, we are going to create the background. Select the&nbsp;<strong>Background&nbsp;</strong>layer, go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; New Fill Layer &gt; Solid Color</strong>&nbsp;to create a new solid color fill layer, name it&nbsp;<strong>Background Color</strong>, and choose the color&nbsp;<code>#e96688</code>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-solid-color-fill-layer-6.jpg" alt="creating new solid color fill layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now&nbsp;<strong>Right-click</strong>&nbsp;on this layer, choose&nbsp;<strong>Blending Options</strong>, check&nbsp;<strong>Gradient Overlay</strong>, and use the settings below:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/adding-layer-style-4.jpg" alt="adding layer style"/></figure>

<h2>4.&nbsp;How to Add Glitter Textures</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In this section we are going to add glitter to photo. Select the&nbsp;<strong>Subject</strong>&nbsp;layer, go to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Place Embedded</strong>, select the<strong>&nbsp;</strong>texture from the first texture link, and click&nbsp;<strong>Place</strong>. Then, set the&nbsp;<strong>Width&nbsp;</strong>and&nbsp;<strong>Height</strong>&nbsp;of the texture to&nbsp;<strong>400%</strong>&nbsp;and the&nbsp;<strong>Angle&nbsp;</strong>to&nbsp;<strong>-90°</strong>, and name this layer&nbsp;<strong>Base_Glitter_1</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/placing-texture-5.jpg" alt="how to add glitter in photoshop"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now change the&nbsp;<strong>Blending Mode</strong>&nbsp;of this layer to&nbsp;<strong>Screen</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/changing-blending-mode-7.jpg" alt="changing blending mode"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; Layer Mask &gt; Reveal All&nbsp;</strong>to add a layer mask that reveals the whole layer. Then, set the foreground color to&nbsp;<code>#000000</code>, choose the&nbsp;<strong>Brush Tool (B)</strong>, pick a soft brush, and brush as shown below. Feel free to use different settings for the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;of the brush.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/brushing-onto-layer-mask-4.jpg" alt="brushing onto layer mask"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now press&nbsp;<strong>Control-J</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to duplicate this layer. Then, go to&nbsp;<strong>Edit &gt; Transform &gt; Flip Horizontal</strong>&nbsp;to flip this layer horizontally. After that, using the&nbsp;<strong>Move Tool (V)</strong>, click and drag anywhere inside the canvas to position the layer as shown below.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/duplicating-layer-5.jpg" alt="duplicating layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Name this layer&nbsp;<strong>Base_Glitter_2&nbsp;</strong>and drag it just below the&nbsp;<strong>Base_Glitter_1</strong>&nbsp;layer in the&nbsp;<strong>Layers&nbsp;</strong>panel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/naming-layer-5.jpg" alt="naming layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:nextpage -->
<!--nextpage-->
<!-- /wp:nextpage -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 6</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now select the&nbsp;<strong>Base_Glitter_1</strong>&nbsp;layer, go to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Place Embedded</strong>, select the<strong>&nbsp;</strong>texture from the second texture link, and click&nbsp;<strong>Place</strong>. Then, set the&nbsp;<strong>Width&nbsp;</strong>and&nbsp;<strong>Height</strong>&nbsp;of the texture to&nbsp;<strong>200%</strong>&nbsp;as shown below, and name this layer&nbsp;<strong>Add_Glitter_1</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/placing-texture-2-4.jpg" alt="add glitter to photo"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 7</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Change the&nbsp;<strong>Blending Mode</strong>&nbsp;of this layer to&nbsp;<strong>Lighten</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/changing-blending-mode-2-4.jpg" alt="changing blending mode"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 8</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; New Fill Layer &gt; Solid Color</strong>&nbsp;to create a new solid color fill layer, name it&nbsp;<strong>A_G_1_Color</strong>, and choose the color&nbsp;<code>#e96688</code>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-solid-color-fill-layer-2-5.jpg" alt="creating new solid color fill layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 9</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Press&nbsp;<strong>Control-Alt-G&nbsp;</strong>on your keyboard to create a clipping mask. Then, change the&nbsp;<strong>Blending Mode&nbsp;</strong>of this layer to&nbsp;<strong>Color</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-clipping-mask-4.jpg" alt="creating clipping mask"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 10</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now&nbsp;<strong>Control-click</strong>&nbsp;on the&nbsp;<strong>Add_Glitter_1</strong>&nbsp;layer to select both layers at the same time. Then, press&nbsp;<strong>Control-J</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to duplicate these layers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/duplicating-layers-4.jpg" alt="duplicating layers"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 11</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the&nbsp;<strong>Add_Glitter_1_copy</strong>&nbsp;layer, and using the&nbsp;<strong>Move Tool (V)</strong>, click and drag anywhere inside the canvas to position this layer as you like.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/positioning-layer-6.jpg" alt="positioning layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:nextpage -->
<!--nextpage-->
<!-- /wp:nextpage -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 12</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now press&nbsp;<strong>Control-T</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to transform this layer, and set the&nbsp;<strong>Angle</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>-30</strong><strong>°</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/transforming-layer-4.jpg" alt="transforming layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 13</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Control-click</strong>&nbsp;on the&nbsp;<strong>A_G_1_Color copy</strong>&nbsp;layer to select both layers at the same time. Then, drag these layers just below the&nbsp;<strong>Add_Glitter_1</strong>&nbsp;layer in the&nbsp;<strong>Layers&nbsp;</strong>panel. After that, name these two layers according to their sequence number.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/arranging-layers-4.jpg" alt="arranging layers"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 14</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now repeat this process to create as many glitter layers as you want. Feel free to use different transform settings for each of the texture layers. Here is my result:</p>

<h2>5.&nbsp;How to Make the Final Adjustments</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In this section we are going to make some final adjustments to the design. Select the&nbsp;<strong>Subject</strong>&nbsp;layer and press&nbsp;<strong>Control-J</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to duplicate it. Then, drag this layer to the top of the layers in the&nbsp;<strong>Layers&nbsp;</strong>panel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/duplicating-layer-2-4.jpg" alt="duplicating layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now press&nbsp;<strong>Control-Shift-U</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to desaturate this layer. Then, change the&nbsp;<strong>Blending Mode</strong>&nbsp;of this layer to&nbsp;<strong>Soft Light&nbsp;</strong>and set the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>73%</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/changing-blending-mode-and-opacity-5.jpg" alt="changing blending mode and opacity"/></figure>

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-gradient-map-adjustment-layer-6.jpg" alt="creating new gradient map adjustment layer"/></figure>
