<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key concepts</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>HTML stands for HyperText Markup Language. It uses elements to describe the role of content in a page. A heading identifies a title or section heading; a paragraph identifies a block of text; an article or section can group related content.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Semantic HTML helps browsers interpret a page and supports assistive technology, search engines, and future maintenance. Choose a tag because it fits the content’s role—not simply because its default appearance looks right.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>HTML describes structure and meaning, CSS controls presentation, and JavaScript adds behavior.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
