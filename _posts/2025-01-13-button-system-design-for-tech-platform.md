<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Design Scenario</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">As a UI designer, your challenge is to craft a button system for a tech platform of your choice. Consider the number of button priorities the system requires, showcasing the various button types and states.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Design Task</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Your design task is to complete the following:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Choose a tech platform and the device type for your design.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Develop a button system that features varying button priorities and showcases button states.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Make sure the button system clearly outlines button parameters.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Ensure the buttons meet the accessibility color contrast requirements.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Offer an explanation for your design choices.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Instructions</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Read the instructions to ensure your submission receives maximum visibility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">You can edit your project at any time.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Remember to share your submission with your community. The more reviews and views it receives, the quicker it will be seen by Uxcel Mentors.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">After submitting your project, consider reviewing others' projects. This can enhance your project's discoverability.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Ensure you only submit your own work.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Evaluation criteria</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your project will be evaluated using the following criteria:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Visual design</strong> — Do designs effectively engage the user through strategically using color, typography, and imagery?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Accessibility—Does the design comply with the WCAG’s requirements and is accessible to all users? </strong>Is it inclusive of all users regardless of their background, race, age, gender, and ethnicity?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Presentation</strong> — How effectively is the work presented, including the templates provided, the formatting of the copy, the links shared, and the explanation of the design rationale?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
