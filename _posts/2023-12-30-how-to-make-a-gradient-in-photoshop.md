<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today let's take a quick look at how to make a gradient in Photoshop using the new and improved Gradient tool. The Photoshop gradient tool got a huge update in 2024, making it completely non-destructive and live. You can easily learn how to add a gradient in Photoshop to an existing image! Let's take a look.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-40ng-how-to-make-a-gradient-in-photoshop">How to Create a Gradient in Photoshop</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-zxs6-step-1">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The&nbsp;<strong>Gradient</strong>&nbsp;tool can be found in the left-hand toolbar or by pressing&nbsp;<strong>G.</strong>&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/tool_2-2.jpg" alt="The Gradient tool can be found in the left hand toolbar"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-skzh-step-2">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the upper&nbsp;<strong>Options</strong>&nbsp;bar, you can select a&nbsp;<strong>Preset</strong>&nbsp;gradient and the&nbsp;<strong>Style,</strong>&nbsp;although these can also be changed later.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To create the initial gradient, click and drag on the canvas. In the below example, I dragged from top to bottom.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0006_2.jpg" alt="To create the initial Gradient click and drag on the canvas"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-0p2u-step-3">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can adjust the placement and angle of the gradient by clicking and dragging the outer Anchor Points and Path.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can come back and edit the placement, style, and color of a Gradient layer for as long as the Gradient layer is&nbsp;<em>not</em>&nbsp;rasterized. Gradient Fill layers are considered non-destructive.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0005_3.jpg" alt="adjust the placement and angle of the gradient by clicking and dragging the outer Anchor Points"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Advertisement</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-gcjo-step-4">Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can adjust the spread of the gradient's color by clicking and dragging the<strong>&nbsp;Color Midpoint,</strong>&nbsp;found between any two colors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In our below example, there are only two colors, so we have one Color Midpoint. A new Color Midpoint will be added between any two colors.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0004_4.jpg" alt="adjust the spread of the gradients' color by clicking and dragging the Color Midpoint"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-93vd-step-5">Step 5&nbsp;</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can find more gradient controls in the&nbsp;<strong>Window &gt; Properties&nbsp;</strong>panel when the<strong>&nbsp;Gradient</strong>&nbsp;layer is active.&nbsp;Here we can choose a new<strong>&nbsp;Preset,</strong>&nbsp;change its&nbsp;<strong>Style,&nbsp;</strong>and place more colors onto our gradient.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To add a new color to your gradient, click right below the<strong>&nbsp;Gradient Bar.</strong>&nbsp;It will create a<strong>&nbsp;Color Stop</strong>&nbsp;in the shade of your&nbsp;<strong>Foreground&nbsp;</strong>color.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To change a color,&nbsp;<strong>double-click</strong>&nbsp;the&nbsp;<strong>Color Stop</strong>&nbsp;to open the&nbsp;<strong>Color Picker&nbsp;</strong>and select a new color.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0003_5.jpg" alt="You can find more gradient controls in the Window &gt; Properties panel"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-d0ma-step-6">Step 6</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To remove a color from a gradient, select the<strong>&nbsp;Color Stop&nbsp;</strong>and hit&nbsp;<strong>Delete.&nbsp; &nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0002_6.jpg" alt="remove a color from a gradient select the Color Stop and hit Delete"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-7dgf-step-7">Step 7</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you need to learn how to add a gradient to an image in Photoshop, here are the details. You can adjust the Opacity of the gradient in different locations using the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity Controls.&nbsp;</strong>The&nbsp;<strong>Opacity Controls&nbsp;</strong>work just like the&nbsp;<strong>Gradient Color Controls.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Select an&nbsp;<strong>Opacity Stop&nbsp;</strong>to adjust its&nbsp;<strong>Opacity.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Move the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity Stop</strong>&nbsp;by clicking and dragging.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Add more&nbsp;<strong>Opacity Stops</strong>&nbsp;by clicking below the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity Controls</strong>&nbsp;bar.&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Delete and&nbsp;<strong>Opacity Stop&nbsp;</strong>by selecting the stop and hitting<strong>&nbsp;Delete.&nbsp;</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0001_7.jpg" alt="adjust the Opacity of the gradient in different locations using the Opacity Controls"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-npd7-thats-how-to-make-a-gradient-in-photoshop">That's How to Make a Gradient in Photoshop</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Photoshop Gradient tool, one of the oldest tools in Photoshop, was long overdue for a refresh. You no longer have to click and undo repeatedly to get the perfect gradient. With the enhanced gradient layer, you can now edit your gradient for as long as you want. Adjust the style, colors, and opacity to your heart's content. Get ready to unleash your creativity and enjoy the newfound flexibility this amazing tool brings!</p>
