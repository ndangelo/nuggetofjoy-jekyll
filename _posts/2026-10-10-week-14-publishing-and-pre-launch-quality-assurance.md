<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key concepts</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before a site is shared, review its content, navigation, responsive layout, forms, images, page metadata, and contact information. Remove placeholder text, check for broken links, and confirm that important interactions work.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Test at more than one viewport width and use keyboard navigation. Make sure images are optimized and text remains readable. If the site is being published publicly, review permissions, ownership, and any personal information included.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A checklist helps you work systematically, but it does not guarantee a perfect experience on every device or for every visitor.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
