<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">About vehicle pre-purchase inspections</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Vehicle’s pre-purchase inspection business is one of the major revenue generating areas under Wrench marketplace. A Standard Inspection includes a comprehensive check of the vehicle’s body, paint, underbody, tires, wheels, glass, electrical items, motor, transmission, and other components.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">Customer places an order through website or by phone. Once you place an order, an expert inspector will contact your seller to verify the availability of the vehicle and set up an appointment to inspect your automobile. The inspector will go directly to the vehicle to perform the inspections.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Lifecycle of an inspection job</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Below points will help you understand the steps involved in a vehicle inspection</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Customer places the order through website or by phone.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Our team of dispatchers will contact our nearest technician and brief them on the automobile as well as any specific concerns a customer may have brought up during the order process ("additional notes").</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Dispatchers will schedule the inspection as soon as possible, based on the seller's or dealer's availability. <em>Inspectors are notified about the job. They can also see the job related details in their inspector portal.</em></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Our inspector will drive directly to the vehicle to perform the inspection. <em>There is an inspection checklist PDF form specific to each type of inspections. It is present in every job card. Inspectors need to carry a printable version of the PDF when they visit the vehicle location.</em></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">At the end of his day, inspector will upload his findings and all the supporting pictures/videos to us. <em>Inspectors login to their inspector portal, upload all the supporting media and fill out all the details in that job checklist page which they captured on the form. After adding all the details, they submit the job for review.</em></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Our skilled review team (ASE Master Technicians) will double-check the findings.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">As soon as the reviewal process is complete, customers will receive an email to let them know that it is time to login and view your report!</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Problem</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">If there are multiple jobs in a day, inspector has to do the same actions multiple times. It ends up inspectors not submitting their findings per job on the same day. This increase the overall turnaround time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Solution</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">To have an inspection app in place which helps our inspectors to upload his findings, click supporting photos / videos and submit the job for review directly from the vehicle location.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">It is an entirely new app for all inspectors to improve operational efficiency and accuracy, accommodating different workflows within the vehicle inspection.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Design Process</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size">After understanding the company's needs and expectations, I designed an application based on the current job lifecycle and created a low-fidelity prototype. I ran user testing with 10 participants and incorporated the feedback to refine the design, resulting in an 80% satisfaction rate. Subsequently, I created a high-fidelity prototype.</p>
