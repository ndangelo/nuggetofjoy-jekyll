<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-white-color has-pale-pink-background-color has-text-color has-background">Research your subject.</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true,"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<ol class="has-medium-font-size"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Write your objective in the <strong>d2l Dropbox</strong> in one sentence.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Define the purpose and function of the poster, the audience, and the information to be</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Find typefaces that express the spirit of the subject. Or use handmade or found type or a combination of handmade and a typeface.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li> Design a poster that is type-driven—text type is the star of the poster. If you use any image, it should play a secondary role to the type-driven headline.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Produce at least twenty sketches. (Must be submitted with final poster on d2l.)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Produce your final poster</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>You can pick the size of your poster <strong>(with prior approval),</strong> or you can choose to use a standard poster size. 2'x3' or 3'x2' .</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-white-color has-pale-pink-background-color has-text-color has-background">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Choose two of your best sketches and refine them.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Establish emphasis through a visual hierarchy.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Carefully examine the spacing between letters, among words, and between lines of type.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3,"backgroundColor":"pale-pink","textColor":"white"} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-white-color has-pale-pink-background-color has-text-color has-background">Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Create a finished solution. This poster is to be completed in<strong> InDesign</strong>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">The poster can be designed in a portrait or landscape orientation.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
