<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-arji-how-to-flip-an-image-in-photoshop">How to Flip an Image in Photoshop</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-sdjg-step-1">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you open your image in Photoshop, you can easily flip it in any direction. To flip your photo horizontally, click on <strong>Image > Image Rotation > Flip Canvas Horizontal </strong>in the upper menu bar. That's how to horizontally flip an image in Photoshop.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Similarly,&nbsp;to&nbsp;flip&nbsp;your&nbsp;image&nbsp;vertically,&nbsp;click&nbsp;on&nbsp;the&nbsp;<strong>Flip Canvas</strong><strong>&nbsp;Vertical</strong>&nbsp;option.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","linkDestination":"custom"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/0005_1_1-3.jpg" alt="go to the top menu and choose Image rotation then flip canvas horizontally "/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-ja86-step-2">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now, if you want to flip just one layer and not the whole canvas, you can select the layer you want to flip and press&nbsp;<strong>Control/Command-T&nbsp;</strong>to bring up the&nbsp;<strong>Transform&nbsp;</strong>controls, which are the blue lines outlining the layer. Now you can<strong>&nbsp;right-click &gt; Flip Horizontal</strong>&nbsp;or<strong>&nbsp;Flip Vertical.&nbsp;</strong>This will flip only the selected layer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-qqhy-how-to-mirror-flip-and-image">How to Mirror Flip an Image in Photoshop&nbsp;</h2>

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Creating a mirror effect is very simple. First,&nbsp;<strong>Duplicate&nbsp;</strong>your original image using&nbsp;<strong>Control/Command-J.&nbsp;&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Move the duplicate layer to the side of the original on the canvas.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="toc-h2lh-conclusion">Conclusion</h2>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That's how to flip an image horizontally in Photoshop. Mirror flipping images in Photoshop is an easy and fun way to create interesting effects, But sometimes you need to flip an image horizontally, which is luckily just a right-click and Flip away. If you flip your images often, you can set up a keyboard shortcut.</p>
