<h2 id="website-design-examples"><a href="https://collegeinfogeek.com/personal-website-examples/#website-design-examples"></a>Website Design Examples</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next, we move to the design portion of this guide. Many of these sites showcase the work of graphic, product, or UX designers; unsurprisingly, they are well-designed. But others showcase the work of authors, photographers, and thought leaders, showing that you don’t have to be a designer to have a compelling site design. All of them serve as an inspiration.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><a href="https://ednacerrillosdotcom.wordpress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Edna Cerrillos</a></strong></p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li><strong>Minimalism with personality</strong>&nbsp;– The image you see above is exactly what appears when you visit Roxane’s site. The “one n” is also a nice branding touch.</li></ul>
