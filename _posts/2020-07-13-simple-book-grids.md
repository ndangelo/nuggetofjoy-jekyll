<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="a-basic-theory-of-grids">A Basic Theory Of Grids</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Whether you work in print or in web and mobile design, you need to understand the basics of grid theory.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="anatomy-of-a-grid">ANATOMY OF A GRID</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Whether simple or complex, all grids have some common parts:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Format</strong><br>The format is the area in which the design is placed. In a paper book, the format is the page. On the web, the format is the size of the browser window.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Margins</strong><br>Margins are the negative space between the edge of the format and the outer edge of the content.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","width":743,"height":1051} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter is-resized"><img src="https://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/layout-grids-image48-large-opt-11.jpg" alt="Margin for a block of content" style="width:743px;height:1051px"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Margin for a block of content</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Columns and alleys</strong><br>In its the most basic form, a grid is made up of two main components: columns and alleys. Columns are the building blocks of grids. The space between columns is referred to as alleys. Together, columns and alleys take up the horizontal width of the screen.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","width":803,"height":903} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter is-resized"><img src="https://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/layout-grids-image20-800w-opt-5.jpg" alt="The space between columns is referred to as alleys." style="width:803px;height:903px"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">The space between columns is referred to as alleys.</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Modules</strong><br>Modules are individual units of space created from the intersection of columns and rows (i.e. the horizontal equivalents of columns).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center","width":746,"height":638} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter is-resized"><img src="https://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/layout-grids-image51-800w-opt-5.jpg" alt="Modules are units created from the intersection of rows and columns." style="width:746px;height:638px"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Modules are units created from the intersection of rows and columns.</figcaption></figure>
