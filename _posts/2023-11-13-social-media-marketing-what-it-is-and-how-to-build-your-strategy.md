<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background"><strong>Assignment: Social Media Campaign</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the following guidelines to create your own social media campaign for your business. Include the following in your campaign.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"elements":{"link":{"color":{"text":"var:preset|color|vivid-purple"}}}},"textColor":"vivid-purple","fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-vivid-purple-color has-text-color has-link-color has-medium-font-size"><strong>You must use adobe express' calendar functionality to post at least one post a day from 11-21-24 till the end of finals week. Done properly this can be completed in advance. Simply date the time you wish a post to post!</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You will create a unified campaign that spans <strong>Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter and Tiktok.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It could be:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Scavenger hunt (with an incentive)</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Special Deal</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>NonProfit (specific event)</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>or more</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Users should be led from one social media format to another - and then perhaps a location? Who knows?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This campaign must include:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>1. Five Facebook posts.</strong> Two with a professional-looking advertisement. (You can use Adobe CC or Spark.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>2.</strong> <strong>A formal video ad.</strong> - a commercial in Facebook and Instagram - storyboards are needed (Spark is perfect for this)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>3.</strong> <strong>One picture post.</strong> (All Platforms)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>4. Two text-only posts</strong> to X (twitter)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>(text should be included with all posts for all platforms.)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>5. Instagram: Professional Advertisement.</strong> (All Platforms)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>6. Four other "behind-the-scenes pictures" </strong>of your business that people don't normally see. (All Platforms)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>7. Five Tiktok or video posts on other social media platforms</strong>. Informal but with a professional "resolve" frame at the end of each</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>8. All these posts need to lead toward your goal.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>9. Hashtags!</strong> Use them liberally in the post. See presentation.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Create ten 20 #hashtags for your brand.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Create ten hashtags for your campaign.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Incorporate these hashtags in all your social media platforms.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Remember the following:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>1. Be true to your brand</strong>. Each of your campaigns&nbsp;reflects your brand identity and values.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>2. Stay focused</strong>. Each campaign has a single, simple message at its core.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>3. They’re trackable.&nbsp;</strong>Each campaign has a distinct hashtag or channel, which means the companies can&nbsp;measure key indicators of success.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>4. They’re unique to each platform.</strong>&nbsp;Broadcasting identical content across every platform is less effort for your social media team, but it looks sloppy.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>5. They’re timely.</strong>&nbsp;Every campaign should be responsive to current events and activities</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>6. They’re emotional.</strong>&nbsp;Be memorable, people should make you feel something when you see them.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>7. Be original.&nbsp;It would be be</strong>st never to imitate someone else’s successful idea.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
