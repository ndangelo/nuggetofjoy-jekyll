<li class="has-medium-font-size">Then, make an Ace and 3, 5, and 9 numbered cards.</li>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">START CREATING YOUR DECK</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<ul class="has-medium-font-size"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Brainstorm. Create thumbnails of possible solutions using pencil &amp; paper.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pay attention to face card conventions<strong> (vertical orientation, mirror image top &amp; bottom, placement of letter &amp; suit, etc).</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What you choose to draw/illustrate for your king, queen, or jack is pretty wide-open as long as it has a face.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>It could be a person, animal, alien, robot, or something. It could be realistic, highly stylized, graphic, etc.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">ATTENTION</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Remember that the card must look the same when held upside-down.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">How are you going to merge the upper half with the lower half?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Look at actual playing cards to see how they do it.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">GUIDELINES</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">You must use a standard (western-traditional) suit.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">Since the card needs to be the same when turned upside-down, you may want to drag a guide across the middle of the card. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">The challenge is finding a clever way to unite them.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<li class="has-medium-font-size">The choice of style and techniques is entirely yours. (Well... I must approve it)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"textColor":"vivid-purple","fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-vivid-purple-color has-text-color has-medium-font-size"><strong>Note: Please include a layout for the back of your playing cards.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
