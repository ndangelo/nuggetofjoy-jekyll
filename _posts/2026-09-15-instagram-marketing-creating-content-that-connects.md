<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Instagram is a visual platform built around photography, video, personality, conversation, and community. Successful Instagram accounts don't simply promote products—they give audiences a reason to follow, interact, and share.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">What Makes Instagram Different?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Successful Instagram marketing often uses:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Short-form video and Reels</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Strong visual identities</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>People and customers</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Humor and cultural trends</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Influencers and partnerships</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>User-generated content (UGC)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
