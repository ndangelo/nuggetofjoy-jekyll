<h3 class="wp-block-heading">ANALYZING BOOK JACKETS&nbsp;AND MAGAZINE COVERS</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Find three examples of book jackets that express the spirit or personality of their&nbsp;Contents. Justify your choices.</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">BOOK COVER DESIGN SERIES</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Select three books written by different authors. Each book should be from a different time. <!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>1. 1980 - 2023 </strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>2. 1920 - 1960 </strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>3. 1880 - 1900</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Read their works. Research them. How do these books fit together? How do they differ? Be very specific! Example: Your theme shouldn’t be “anger”</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Write an objective statement. Define the purpose and function of the problem, the audience for the books, and the information to be communicated. <strong>The statement should be at least</strong> 1 1/2 pages. (discuss all three and the common thread <strong>between the books.</strong>)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true,"type":"1"} -->
<ol type="1"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Create two small sketches for each book cover. Two for each writer in your series.</strong> Sketch the front covers and spines as well.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The covers must be similar in style and yet express the individuality of each writer.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Create a publisher's logo on each cover in the same position.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Your solution may be purely typographic or visually driven</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Think about the various ways the series could be tied together through the use of similar visuals: illustrations, graphics, photographs, typography,</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>After you have completed the above tasks, submit one or multiple pdfs containing the above content.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Name the series.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Design a logo for the book series.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true,"type":"1"} -->
<ol type="1"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Design&nbsp;the final <strong>three book covers—one for each writer in your series.</strong>&nbsp;Design front covers and spines.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>I will review your first book covers before you proceed with the others.&nbsp;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->
