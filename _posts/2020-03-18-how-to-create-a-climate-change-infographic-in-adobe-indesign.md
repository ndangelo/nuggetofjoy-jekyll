<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Help spread awareness about the effects of global warming with this informative climate change infographic. Easily adaptable to your own choice of facts and stats, this is a great template for creating a unique design.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We’ll set up the layout in&nbsp;<strong>Adobe InDesign</strong>&nbsp;and prepare it for sharing online, on social media or Pinterest.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What You’ll Need to Create Your Infographic</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You’ll need access to&nbsp;<strong>Adobe InDesign</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Adobe Photoshop</strong>&nbsp;to create your design. If you want to create circular pie-chart graphics, you’ll also need to have&nbsp;<strong>Adobe Illustrator</strong>&nbsp;to hand. In addition, you’ll need to download the following images and font files:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><a href="https://pixabay.com/en/bear-polar-polar-bear-nature-1102605/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Polar bear photo</a></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Charlevoix Pro font, in <a href="https://graphicriver.net/item/charlevoix-pro-regular/15144030" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Regular</a> and <a href="https://graphicriver.net/item/charlevoix-pro-bold/13088822" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bold</a> weights (Find another font that you find appropriate.)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Weight vector illustration from <a href="https://graphicriver.net/item/scales-and-rulers-icons/7842879" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">this icon set</a> (You will need to create your own icon.)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you want to customize your design with more icon details, you can use some of the icons in <a href="https://graphicriver.net/item/energy-line-inverted-icons/12834951" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">this line energy set</a> or <a href="https://graphicriver.net/item/40-environment-sustainable-vector-icons/4103558" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">this environment set</a>. (Please create your own or find free alternatives.)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you’ve downloaded the images and installed the fonts on your computer, you’re ready to begin.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1.&nbsp;How to Set Up Your Infographic Document</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open up&nbsp;<strong>InDesign</strong>&nbsp;and go to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; New &gt; Document.</strong>&nbsp;Choose&nbsp;<strong>Web</strong>&nbsp;for the&nbsp;<strong>Intent</strong>&nbsp;at the top, and deselect&nbsp;<strong>Facing Pages</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Under&nbsp;<strong>Page Size</strong>, set the&nbsp;<strong>Width</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>1800 px*</strong>&nbsp;and the&nbsp;<strong>Height</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>6950 px</strong>&nbsp;(you can adjust the height later if you wish by using the&nbsp;<strong>Page Tool</strong>).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.44.44-2.jpg" alt="new document"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>&nbsp;to create your document.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.45.34-2.jpg" alt="document"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Expand the&nbsp;<strong>Layers</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Layers)</strong>, and double-click on&nbsp;<strong><em>Layer 1</em></strong>&nbsp;to open the&nbsp;<strong>Options</strong>&nbsp;window. Rename the layer&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>&nbsp;and click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click on the&nbsp;<strong>Create New Layer&nbsp;</strong>button at the bottom of the panel and rename this second layer&nbsp;<strong><em>Photo</em></strong>. Then create a final new layer called&nbsp;<strong><em>Typography</em></strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.46.31-2.jpg" alt="new layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock all the layers except&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.46.44-2.jpg" alt="layers"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before you begin, it’s a good idea to create a complete color palette for your design. Here’s how.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Expand the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Color &gt; Swatches)</strong>, and select&nbsp;<strong>New Color Swatch</strong>&nbsp;from the panel’s drop-down menu (at top-right).&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the&nbsp;<strong>Type</strong>&nbsp;set to&nbsp;<strong>Process</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Mode</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>RGB</strong>, adjust the levels of&nbsp;<strong>Red, Green</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Blue</strong>&nbsp;until you’ve created your desired result. Click&nbsp;<strong>Add</strong>&nbsp;and then&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.50.45-2.jpg" alt="swatch options"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Repeat this process to create more RGB swatches, building up a full palette. To create the palette used on the design pictured here, create the following swatches:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=251 G=252 B=247</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=224 G=110 B=9</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=164 G=104 B=13</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=118 G=81 B=37</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=229 G=35 B=47</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=168 G=217 B=232</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=83 G=172 B=188</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=45 G=4 B=11</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=116 G=194 B=206</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=47 G=154 B=172</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=0 G=127 B=159</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>R=51 G=52 B=80</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can also create two gradient swatches by choosing&nbsp;<strong>New Gradient Swatch</strong>&nbsp;from the panel menu. Here, we can create one ‘Heat Gradient’ and one ‘Flood Gradient’ to create contrasting effects on the design.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For the&nbsp;<strong>Heat Gradient&nbsp;</strong>swatch, set the&nbsp;<strong>Type</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Radial</strong>&nbsp;and choose&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;for the&nbsp;<strong>Stop Color.</strong>&nbsp;Then choose a darker red swatch for one stop and an orange swatch for the other.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.51.18-2.jpg" alt="heat gradient"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For the&nbsp;<strong>Flood Gradient</strong>&nbsp;swatch, choose a pale blue for one stop and&nbsp;<strong>[Paper]</strong>&nbsp;(white) for the other.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.51.31-2.jpg" alt="flood gradient"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the rulers visible&nbsp;<strong>(View &gt; Show Rulers),</strong>&nbsp;drag a guide down from the top ruler, down to Y position&nbsp;<strong>1865 px</strong>&nbsp;(you can see this in the top&nbsp;<strong>Controls</strong>&nbsp;panel).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Drag a second guide down to <strong>3780 px,</strong> a third to <strong>4540 px</strong>, and a final to 5385 px. This section is up the page and ready to apply our background color. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.53.15-2.jpg" alt="guides"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2.&nbsp;How to Build Up a Background on Your Layout</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Take the&nbsp;<strong>Rectangle Tool (M)</strong>&nbsp;and drag across the top section of the page, extending it down to the first guide. From the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel, set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;to a sky-blue swatch.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.53.45-2.jpg" alt="top blue rectangle"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create more rectangle shapes, filling each designated section with a color, graduating from pale sea-blue to deep blue towards the bottom.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.54.28-2.jpg" alt="swatches"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Zoom into the bottom of the page. Use the&nbsp;<strong>Pen Tool (P)&nbsp;</strong>to draw a rough shape, mimicking the sandy curves of the bottom of the ocean. Set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;to a dark peach shade.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.55.22-2.jpg" alt="sand"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Edit &gt; Copy, Edit &gt; Paste</strong>&nbsp;the shape, and move it below, covering the bottom of the page completely. Adjust the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;to a beige shade.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.55.39-2.jpg" alt="sand"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Scroll up towards the top of the page.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the&nbsp;<strong>Pen Tool (P)</strong>&nbsp;to draw a straight line across the top guide (beneath the first colored section), and then pull it down and work your way across, creating a jagged illustration. This gives the impression of the underneath of an iceberg.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Join the illustration into a complete shape and set the&nbsp;<strong>Fill</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>[Paper].</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.56.34-2.jpg" alt="paper fill"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Expand the&nbsp;<strong>Stroke</strong>&nbsp;panel&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Stroke)</strong>, and set the&nbsp;<strong>Weight</strong>&nbsp;of the stroke to<strong>&nbsp;6 pt&nbsp;</strong>and&nbsp;<strong>Type&nbsp;</strong>to&nbsp;<strong>Right Slant Hash</strong>, to give the design some texture.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.57.10-2.jpg" alt="right slant hash"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Finally, head up to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Effects &gt; Drop Shadow</strong>. Bring the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;down to&nbsp;<strong>10%</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Effect Color&nbsp;</strong>(by clicking on the colored square next to the&nbsp;<strong>Mode</strong>&nbsp;menu at the top) to a slightly contrasting blue shade to make the shadow appear subtle and natural.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2015.58.25-2.jpg" alt="effect color"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>&nbsp;to exit.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3.&nbsp;How to Edit the Photo for Your Infographic</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Go to File > Save to save your design. Then, minimize the window for a moment. Open the <a href="https://pixabay.com/en/bear-polar-polar-bear-nature-1102605/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">polar bear photo</a> in <strong>Photoshop</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Duplicate the&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>&nbsp;layer to keep a copy of the image intact, and switch off its visibility.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then use the&nbsp;<strong>Lasso Tool (L)&nbsp;</strong>to section off an area around the edge of the bear’s silhouette. The aim is to isolate the image from its background.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.02.43-2.jpg" alt="lasso tool"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you’ve selected an area, click on the&nbsp;<strong>Refine Edge</strong>&nbsp;button in the top&nbsp;<strong>Controls</strong>&nbsp;panel, and check&nbsp;<strong>Smart Radius</strong>. Adjust the sliders until you’re happy with the accuracy of the selection, before clicking&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.03.24-2.jpg" alt="refine edge"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then hit&nbsp;<strong>Delete</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to remove the selection.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.03.43-2.jpg" alt="cut out section"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Work your way around the whole image until the background is removed. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The image will look more stylish in the final layout if we convert it to black and white.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Head up to&nbsp;<strong>Image &gt; Adjustments &gt; Channel Mixer.</strong>&nbsp;Check&nbsp;<strong>Monochrome</strong>&nbsp;at the bottom of the window, and adjust the levels until you’re happy with the result. Click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.05.16-2.jpg" alt="channel mixer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Save As&nbsp;</strong>your image as a&nbsp;<strong>Photoshop (.psd)</strong>&nbsp;file, which will preserve the transparent background and the quality of the image.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Return to your&nbsp;<strong>InDesign</strong>&nbsp;document and lock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Background</em></strong>&nbsp;layer. Unlock the layer above,&nbsp;<strong><em>Photo</em></strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the<strong> Rectangle Frame Tool (F)</strong> to create an image frame over the top of the iceberg illustration and the bottom of the sky-blue-colored section.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>File &gt; Place</strong>, choose your&nbsp;<strong>Photoshop</strong>&nbsp;image, and&nbsp;<strong>Open</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.06.43-2.jpg" alt="photo layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the image frame selected, go to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Effects &gt; Transparency</strong>, and set the&nbsp;<strong>Mode</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Hard Light.</strong>&nbsp;Click&nbsp;<strong>OK</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.07.16-2.jpg" alt="hard light"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then select the image frame and&nbsp;<strong>Edit &gt; Copy, Edit &gt; Paste in Place</strong>, creating a second copy of the image layered over the top. Head back to the&nbsp;<strong>Effects</strong>&nbsp;window again, and adjust the&nbsp;<strong>Mode</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Normal</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>50%</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.07.43-2.jpg" alt="transparency"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">4.&nbsp;How to Format Typography for Your Infographic</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lock the&nbsp;<strong><em>Photo</em></strong>&nbsp;layer and unlock the top layer,&nbsp;<strong><em>Typography</em></strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the <strong>Type Tool (T)</strong> to create a text frame at the top of the page, and type in the infographic's title. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From either the&nbsp;<strong>Controls</strong>&nbsp;panel at the top of the workspace or the&nbsp;<strong>Character</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Paragraph</strong>&nbsp;panels&nbsp;<strong>(Window &gt; Type &amp; Tables &gt; Character)</strong>, set the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Charlevoix Pro Bold, Size 140 pt, Tracking 40</strong>,&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Align Center.</strong>&nbsp;Adjust the&nbsp;<strong>Font Color</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>[Paper]&nbsp;</strong>from the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.10.08-2.jpg" alt="text frame"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To create a border around the title, click on the text frame with the&nbsp;<strong>Selection Tool (V, Escape)</strong>, and set the&nbsp;<strong>Stroke</strong>&nbsp;of the frame to&nbsp;<strong>[Paper]&nbsp;</strong>from the&nbsp;<strong>Swatches</strong>&nbsp;panel. Then go to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Text Frame Options&nbsp;</strong>and increase the&nbsp;<strong>Inset</strong>&nbsp;value (here, to&nbsp;<strong>30 px</strong>) until the text is nicely centered in the frame.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.10.45-2.jpg" alt="text frame options"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you wish, you can also create a text frame below the main title for a subtitle. Set the text in <strong>Charlevoix Pro Regular.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create another text frame and layer over the top of the polar bear photo. Type in&nbsp;<em>‘2050’</em>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Font Color&nbsp;</strong>to&nbsp;<strong>Heat Gradient.</strong>&nbsp;Set the&nbsp;<strong>Font</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Bebas Neue</strong>&nbsp;and make the&nbsp;<strong>Font Size&nbsp;</strong>very generous (around<strong>&nbsp;600 pt</strong>).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can also adjust the&nbsp;<strong>Transparency</strong>&nbsp;settings in the&nbsp;<strong>Effects</strong>&nbsp;window to&nbsp;<strong>Multiply</strong>, to bring through some of the photo beneath.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.11.36-2.jpg" alt="heat gradient"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a text frame to the top left of the polar bear, and type in your first fact. Of course, you can adapt this to whatever statistic you’d like to present. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set the <strong>Font</strong> to <strong>Charlevoix Pro </strong>and play with the proportions of the text, adjusting the <strong>Font Size, Leading </strong>(line-spacing), and <strong>Font Color </strong>to create a hierarchy in the paragraph and draw out keywords. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.12.23-2.jpg" alt="text highlighted"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can create ‘pointers’ for items of text by using the&nbsp;<strong>Line Tool ()</strong>. In the&nbsp;<strong>Stroke</strong>&nbsp;panel, set the&nbsp;<strong>Type</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>CircleSolid</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.13.12-2.jpg" alt="circle solid"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Add credits for each statistic in a separate text frame, set in a smaller&nbsp;<strong>Font Size.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.13.52-2.jpg" alt="text frame"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can create arrows by using the Line Tool () and Stroke panel, too, though I generally prefer to use the Line Tool to create both the stem and arrowhead, allowing me to control the exact proportions of the arrow. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.16.28-2.jpg" alt="arrow"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here I’ve created an arrow to the right side of the polar bear, before setting a statistic in&nbsp;<strong>Charlevoix Pro Bold</strong>&nbsp;in a text frame next to it. I’ve adjusted the&nbsp;<strong>Font Color&nbsp;</strong>to&nbsp;<strong>Heat Gradient</strong>&nbsp;for extra impact.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.18.22-2.jpg" alt="heat gradient"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I continue to build up text around this key statistic, set in more pared-back&nbsp;<strong>Charlevoix Pro Regular</strong>&nbsp;and a&nbsp;<strong>[Paper] Font Color.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.20.03-2.jpg" alt="text frame"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 6</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you’ve designed one statistic, you can use this as a model for creating others by copying and pasting. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I’ve pasted the arrow and text frames onto the center of the iceberg section and adjusted the text content and<strong> Font Color. </strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.21.59-2.jpg" alt="text frame"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you’re completely happy with the formatting and arrangement of the text, you can head to Type > Create Outlines to vectorise it*. This allows you to scale the group easily to suit your design. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>*Please note, this will prevent you from applying further formatting, like adjusting tracking etc., so it might be sensible to paste a copy of your original text onto the artboard next to the page.&nbsp;</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.23.40-2.jpg" alt="create outlines"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 7</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can add icons to your infographic quickly and easily by opening up a vector set of icons in Illustrator, before copying and pasting over individual icons.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Create a weight icon in InDesign before adjusting the<strong> Fill</strong> to an off-white swatch. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.25.45-2.jpg" alt="weight icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Embellish your icon designs with arrows and text, and pull out key figures or words in a contrasting color.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.26.28-2.jpg" alt="statistic"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 8</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can also create section arrow icons by using the&nbsp;<strong>Ellipse Tool (L)</strong>&nbsp;and holding&nbsp;<strong>Shift</strong>&nbsp;to create a small circle towards the bottom of a colored section.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.27.22-2.jpg" alt="circle"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the&nbsp;<strong>Pen Tool (P)</strong>&nbsp;to create a simple arrow shape inside the circle.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.27.35-2.jpg" alt="arrow head"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Right-Click &gt; Group</strong>&nbsp;the elements together to create an icon that's easy to copy and paste.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.28.08-2.jpg" alt="group"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Continue to build up statistics down the length of your infographic, varying the&nbsp;<strong>Font Colors&nbsp;</strong>for contrast and interest.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.28.40-2.jpg" alt="statistic"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 9</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To create a pie chart, we’ll need the brief help of Illustrator. Minimize your InDesign document, and open up&nbsp;<strong>Illustrator</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the&nbsp;<strong>Ellipse Tool (L)&nbsp;</strong>and hold&nbsp;<strong>Shift</strong>&nbsp;to create a circle on the artboard, setting it to have no fill and a thick stroke (around&nbsp;<strong>50 pt</strong>).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.35.39-2.jpg" alt="circle"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Go to&nbsp;<strong>Object &gt; Path &gt; Outline Stroke.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.36.15-2.jpg" alt="outline stroke"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then head up to&nbsp;<strong>Edit &gt; Copy.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 10</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Please return to your <strong>InDesign</strong> document and <strong>Edit > Paste </strong>the circle onto the page, setting it in the center of a colored section. Adjust the color to a pale swatch.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.36.40-2.jpg" alt="white circle"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Edit &gt; Paste, Edit &gt; Paste in Place</strong>&nbsp;the circle directly on top of the first, and adjust the color to a contrasting swatch.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.39.46-2.jpg" alt="brown circle"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then take the&nbsp;<strong>Scissors Tool (C)</strong>&nbsp;and snip away at the sides of the circle on top, isolating a section of the ‘pie’ to roughly match the percentage of your statistic.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.41.24-2.jpg" alt="cut out section"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"align":"center"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.42.11-2.jpg" alt="percentage"/></figure>

<p>You can also add bar charts to your infographic, which are easier to do directly in InDesign.&nbsp;</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>First, use the&nbsp;<strong>Line Tool ()</strong>&nbsp;to create two arrows, one pointing upwards and the other pointing right.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Then use the <strong>Rectangle Tool (M) </strong>to create ‘bars’ on the chart. You can soften these corners by going to <strong>Object > Corner Options </strong>and setting the <strong>Shape</strong> to <strong>Rounded</strong>. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">5.&nbsp;How to Export Your Design for Web</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When you’ve finished your infographic design, make sure to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Save</strong>&nbsp;all your hard work.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To export the design ready for sharing online, head up to&nbsp;<strong>File &gt; Export.&nbsp;</strong>Choose&nbsp;<strong>JPEG</strong>&nbsp;or&nbsp;<strong>PNG</strong>&nbsp;from the&nbsp;<strong>Format</strong>&nbsp;menu at the bottom of the window, and give the image a suitable name.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.45.55-2.jpg" alt="png export"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click&nbsp;<strong>Save</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the export options window, you can adjust the image settings to make it suitable for uploading online. Adjust the Quality to Medium or High, set the Resolution to 72 ppi, and ensure the <strong>Color Space</strong> is set to <strong>RGB</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/Screen20Shot202017-11-2120at2016.46.10-2.jpg" alt="export png"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Finally, click&nbsp;<strong>Export</strong>&nbsp;to create your web-ready image.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Awesome job! Your infographic is finished!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
