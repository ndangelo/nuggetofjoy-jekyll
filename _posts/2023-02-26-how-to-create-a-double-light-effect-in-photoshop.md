<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>1.&nbsp;How to Get Started</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>First, open the photo that you want to work with. To open your photo, go to <strong>File > Open</strong>, choose your photo, and click <strong>Open</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Your photo should be in&nbsp;<strong>RGB Color</strong>&nbsp;mode,&nbsp;<strong>8 Bits/Channel</strong>. To check this, go to&nbsp;<strong>Image &gt; Mode</strong>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>For best results, your photo size should be&nbsp;<strong>2000–4000 px wide/high</strong>. To check this, go to<strong>&nbsp;Image &gt; Image Size</strong>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Your photo should be the&nbsp;<strong>Background</strong>&nbsp;layer. If it is not, go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; New &gt; Background from Layer</strong>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/checking-image-mode-and-size-3.jpg" alt="checking image mode and size"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>2. How to Select the Subject in Photoshop</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In this section, we are going to make a selection of our subject and then copy the subject to a separate layer. Choose the&nbsp;<strong>Quick Selection Tool</strong>&nbsp;<strong>(W)</strong>&nbsp;and select the background of the photo. Use the&nbsp;<strong>Shift-Alt&nbsp;</strong>buttons on your keyboard to add or subtract areas from the selection. After you've made a perfect selection, press&nbsp;<strong>Control-Shift-I</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to invert the selection.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/making-selection-3.jpg" alt="making selection"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now go to&nbsp;<strong>Select &gt; Modify &gt; Smooth</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Sample Radius</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>5 px</strong>. Next, go to&nbsp;<strong>Select &gt; Modify &gt; Contract</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;set&nbsp;<strong>Contract By</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>1 px</strong>. Finally, go to&nbsp;<strong>Select &gt; Modify &gt; Feather&nbsp;</strong>and set the&nbsp;<strong>Feather Radius</strong>&nbsp;to<strong>&nbsp;1 px</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/modifying-selection-3.jpg" alt="modifying selection"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Press&nbsp;<strong>Control-J</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to create a new layer using the selection. Name this new layer&nbsp;<strong>Subject</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-layer-using-selection-7.jpg" alt="creating new layer using selection"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>3.&nbsp;How to Create a Double Light Photoshop Effect</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In this section, we are going to create a double light effect. Go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; New Adjustment Layer &gt; Black &amp; White</strong>&nbsp;to create a new black &amp; white adjustment layer, and name it&nbsp;<strong>Subject_Saturation</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-black-and-white-adjustment-layer-2.jpg" alt="creating new black and white adjustment layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now press&nbsp;<strong>Control-Alt-G</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to create a clipping mask. Then, change the&nbsp;<strong>Blending Mode</strong>&nbsp;of this layer to&nbsp;<strong>Color</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>50%</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/changing-blending-mode-and-opacity-8.jpg" alt="changing blending mode and opacity"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; New Fill Layer &gt; Solid Color</strong>&nbsp;to create a new solid color fill layer, name it&nbsp;<strong>Left Light</strong>, and choose the color&nbsp;<code>#ff0000</code>&nbsp;as shown below:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-solid-color-fill-layer-9.jpg" alt="creating new solid color fill layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now change the&nbsp;<strong>Blending Mode</strong>&nbsp;of this layer to&nbsp;<strong>Overlay</strong>, set the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>75%&nbsp;</strong>and press&nbsp;<strong>Control-Alt-G</strong>&nbsp;to create a clipping mask. Then, select the layer mask of this layer and press&nbsp;<strong>Control-I</strong>&nbsp;to invert its color. After that, set the foreground color to&nbsp;<code>#ffffff</code>, choose the&nbsp;<strong>Brush Tool (B)</strong>, pick a soft brush, and brush over the photo where you want to reveal the left light.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/brushing-into-layer-mask-2-2.jpg" alt="brushing into layer mask"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; New Fill Layer &gt; Solid Color</strong>&nbsp;to create a new solid color fill layer, name it&nbsp;<strong>Right Light</strong>, and choose the color&nbsp;<code>#0080ff</code>&nbsp;as shown below. Then, drag it just below the&nbsp;<strong>Left Light</strong>&nbsp;layer in the&nbsp;<strong>Layers</strong>&nbsp;panel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-solid-color-fill-layer-2-8.jpg" alt="creating new solid color fill layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 6</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now change the&nbsp;<strong>Blending Mode</strong>&nbsp;of this layer to&nbsp;<strong>Overlay</strong>, set the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>75%</strong>. Then, select the layer mask of this layer and press&nbsp;<strong>Control-I</strong>&nbsp;on your keyboard to invert its color. After that, set the foreground color to&nbsp;<code>#ffffff</code>, choose the&nbsp;<strong>Brush Tool (B)</strong>, pick a soft brush, and brush over the photo where you want to reveal the right light.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/brushing-into-layer-mask-3-2.jpg" alt="brushing into layer mask"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 7</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Right-click</strong>&nbsp;on the&nbsp;<strong>Left Light</strong>&nbsp;layer, choose&nbsp;<strong>Blending Options</strong>&nbsp;and make the adjustments below. Then, repeat this for the&nbsp;<strong>Right Light</strong>&nbsp;layer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/adjusting-blending-options-3.jpg" alt="adjusting blending options"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>4.&nbsp;How to Create the Glow</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In this section, we are going to create the double light glow.&nbsp;<strong>Alt-click</strong>&nbsp;on the&nbsp;<strong>Subject</strong>&nbsp;layer and hold and drag the layer to create a new duplicated layer at the top of the layers in the&nbsp;<strong>Layers</strong>&nbsp;panel. Then, while this new layer is selected, go to&nbsp;<strong>Filter &gt; Filter Gallery &gt; Stylize &gt; Glowing Edges</strong>, and set the&nbsp;<strong>Edge Width</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>2</strong>,&nbsp;<strong>Edge Brightness</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>20</strong>, and<strong>&nbsp;Smoothness</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>15</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/adding-glowing-edges-filter-2.jpg" alt="adding glowing edges filter"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now press&nbsp;<strong>Control-Shift-U</strong>&nbsp;to desaturate this layer. Then, go to&nbsp;<strong>Filter &gt; Blur &gt; Gaussian Blur</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Radius</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>10 px</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/adding-gaussian-blur-filter-2.jpg" alt="adding gaussian blur filter"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Right-click</strong>&nbsp;on this layer, choose&nbsp;<strong>Blending Options</strong>&nbsp;and make the adjustments below.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/adjusting-blending-options-2-2.jpg" alt="adjusting blending options"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now change the&nbsp;<strong>Blending Mode</strong>&nbsp;of this layer to&nbsp;<strong>Linear Dodge (Add)</strong>&nbsp;and name it&nbsp;<strong>Subject Glow</strong>. Then, select the&nbsp;<strong>Left Light</strong>&nbsp;layer and&nbsp;<strong>Control-click</strong>&nbsp;on the&nbsp;<strong>Right Light</strong>&nbsp;layer to select both layers at the same time. After that,&nbsp;<strong>Alt-click</strong>&nbsp;on any of these two layers and hold and drag the layers to create new duplicated layers just above the&nbsp;<strong>Subject Glow</strong>&nbsp;layer in the&nbsp;<strong>Layers</strong>&nbsp;panel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/duplicating-layers-7.jpg" alt="duplicating layers"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>While these two layers are selected, press&nbsp;<strong>Control-Shift-G</strong>&nbsp;to create clipping masks. Then, change their&nbsp;<strong>Blending Mode</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>Color</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>25%</strong>. After that, rename the&nbsp;<strong>Left Light copy</strong>&nbsp;layer to&nbsp;<strong>Left Glow</strong>&nbsp;and the&nbsp;<strong>Right Light copy</strong>&nbsp;layer to<strong>&nbsp;Right Glow</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/changing-blending-mode-and-opacity-2-7.jpg" alt="changing blending mode and opacity"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>5.&nbsp;How to Make the Final Adjustments</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 1</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In this section, we are going to make the final adjustments to the effect. Select the&nbsp;<strong>Background</strong>&nbsp;layer, go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; New Fill Layer &gt; Gradient Fill&nbsp;</strong>to create a new gradient fill layer, name it<strong>&nbsp;Background Tint</strong>, and use the settings below:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-gradient-fill-layer-2.jpg" alt="creating new gradient fill layer"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">The left color stop has the color #ff0000 and the right color stop has the color #0080ff.</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 2</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now change the&nbsp;<strong>Blending Mode</strong>&nbsp;of this layer to&nbsp;<strong>Color</strong>&nbsp;and set the&nbsp;<strong>Opacity</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>20%</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/changing-blending-mode-and-opacity-3-3.jpg" alt="changing blending mode and opacity"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 3</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select the&nbsp;<strong>Left Glow</strong>&nbsp;layer, go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; New Adjustment Layer &gt; Levels&nbsp;</strong>to create a new levels adjustment layer, and name it&nbsp;<strong>Overall Brightness</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-levels-adjustment-layer-3.jpg" alt="creating new levels adjustment layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 4</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now&nbsp;<strong>Double-click</strong>&nbsp;on this layer thumbnail and, in the&nbsp;<strong>Properties</strong>&nbsp;panel, enter the settings below:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/adjusting-levels-2.jpg" alt="adjusting levels"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 5</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Go to&nbsp;<strong>Layer &gt; New Adjustment Layer &gt; Vibrance</strong>&nbsp;to create a new vibrance adjustment layer and name it&nbsp;<strong>Overall Vibrance/Saturation</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/creating-new-vibrance-and-saturation-adjustment-layer-2.jpg" alt="creating new vibrance and saturation adjustment layer"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Step 6</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Now&nbsp;<strong>Double-click</strong>&nbsp;on this layer thumbnail and, in the<strong>&nbsp;Properties</strong>&nbsp;panel, set the&nbsp;<strong>Vibrance</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>+20</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>Saturation</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>+10</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="http://nuggetofjoy.com/wp-content/uploads/adjusting-vibrance-and-saturation-2.jpg" alt="adjusting vibrance and saturation"/></figure>
