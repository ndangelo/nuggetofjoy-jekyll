<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key concepts</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>AI tools can help generate ideas, organize a brief, or suggest possible approaches. The quality of output depends partly on the clarity of the request, but even a detailed prompt can produce inaccurate, generic, inaccessible, or impractical suggestions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A useful workflow defines the audience and goal, sets constraints, requests alternatives, evaluates the results, and tests promising ideas. Never assume generated code is secure, accessible, accurate, or production-ready without reviewing it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The responsibility for the final design and its consequences remains with the designer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
