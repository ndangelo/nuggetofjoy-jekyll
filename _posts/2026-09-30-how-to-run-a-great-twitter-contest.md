<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A good X contest or giveaway should be <strong>simple, easy to understand, visually engaging, and designed as a campaign—not just one post.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For this assignment, you will create an <strong>8-post X contest campaign</strong>. Each post should have a different purpose while maintaining a consistent visual style.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before creating your campaign, review X's current contest guidelines</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-contest-rules%5B/embedpress">https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-contest-rules</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":1,"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber"} -->
<h1 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">1. Start With a Goal</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>First, decide what your contest is supposed to accomplish.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Possible goals include:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Increase engagement</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Promote a product</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Build brand awareness</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Generate user-created content</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Increase followers</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Drive traffic to a website</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Example</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Goal:</strong> Increase engagement with college-age sneaker fans while promoting a new sneaker.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The goal should influence everything else you create.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":1,"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber"} -->
<h1 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">2. Know Your Audience</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Be specific about who you are trying to reach.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Too broad:</strong><br>College students</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Better:</strong><br>College students ages 18–24 who are interested in sneakers, fashion, and streetwear.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your prize should also make sense for your audience.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Example</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Contest:</strong> Design Your Dream Sneaker</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Prize:</strong> The winning sneaker design will actually be produced.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The prize gives the audience a reason to participate because it is directly connected to their interests.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":1,"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber"} -->
<h1 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">3. Keep the Rules Simple</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>People should be able to understand how to enter almost immediately.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Example</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>HOW TO ENTER</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Create your sneaker design.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Post it on X.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Mention @YourBrand.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Use #DreamSneakerContest.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Avoid making the entry process unnecessarily complicated.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":1,"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber"} -->
<h1 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">4. Create a Campaign — Not Just One Post</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your eight posts should work together as a campaign.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Don't simply make eight versions of the same advertisement.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Instead, give each post a different job:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Launch → Prize → Instructions → Engagement → User Content → Behind the Scenes → Reminder → Final Call</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":1,"backgroundColor":"luminous-vivid-amber"} -->
<h1 class="wp-block-heading has-luminous-vivid-amber-background-color has-background">Post 1: Launch</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Purpose</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Introduce the contest and immediately communicate:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What the contest is</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What the prize is</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>How long people have to enter</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Example</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>CONTEST TIME!</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Design your dream sneaker for a chance to have your design produced.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Enter by October 15.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>#DreamSneakerContest</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Real X Example</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This Throne giveaway is a good example of a launch post because the giveaway, prize, and limited time are immediately communicated.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
